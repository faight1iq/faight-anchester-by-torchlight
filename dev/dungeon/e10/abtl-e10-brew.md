{{atbl-keeper-summary
##### The Rite in the Deer Park
The lights across the lake are a ritual. Hester Crayle, the Silverbrook midwife, and five villagers have carried a young woman in labour into the deer park. Her name is Edie Lamb. She is not Claudia Bay, and Hester is not the Clairvoyant, but the players do not know that until the fight is over. Five lamps hold the circle open. The rite is a trial of the one the Clairvoyant means to use on herself: when the child is born, the birth opens a door, and the Outsider that comes through crosses into Hester. The players must break the circle or kill Hester before the birth, and bring the mother and child through the night.
}}

### Timeline
{{noIndent
**Weeks ago.** Hester Crayle, midwife to half of Silverbrook, took Edie Lamb on as her patient and told her the big house would see to her lying-in.

**Days ago.** Colonel Standard brought Claudia Bay to Claremonte Gardens. She and Edie were kept together in the Locked Room.

**Yesterday.** A dragonborn assassin killed Standard at the gala. The players took his Pale Journal and the gate key.

**This evening.** Claudia left for town in the closed carriage. Edie's labour began. The household carried her down to the deer park.

**Ten o'clock.** Hester lights the five lamps on the Moon Mound. **Tonight.**
}}

\column

### Episode Structure
The episode starts at the second-floor window where Episode Nine ended. The players cross the lake, or go round it, and find the ritual in the deer park, a maze of glades called the Beastwood.

The fight on the Moon Mound is the episode. It runs against the Crossing Track: one step a round. The players stop the ritual by putting out all five lamps or by killing Hester. If the track reaches six, the birth opens the door and a Greater Eye Fluke drops through. Unless the players kill it in the round it lands, it merges with Hester, and Hester Ascended turns on Edie and the child.

After the fight, the players deliver the baby. Then they find Hester's bag: the instructions for the rite in the Clairvoyant's hand, and Hester's report to a Mr. Connely. The woman on the mound is not Claudia. Claudia went to town this evening.

### Scene 1: Across the Lake
***Claremonte Gardens, the lake shore. Night. ~15 min***

{{descriptive
A flight of steps runs down from the terrace to a boathouse on the water. The lake is black and still. On the far side, lights move among the trees.
}}

**What's Happening.** The ritual began at ten. Set the Crossing Track by how Episode Nine ended: **0** for total success, **1** for mixed, **2** for total failure.

**The Boat.** A rowing boat in the boathouse crosses the lake in a few minutes and lands at the Landing (W1). The water is open and the moon is up.

**The Path.** A gravel path runs round the east end of the lake and enters the deer park at the Lake Gap (W2). It is dark under the trees and no one watches it, but the walk is long: add 1 to the Crossing Track.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Stealth DC 16.** Cross the lake unseen. On a failure, the Pike brothers see the boat from the South Hollow (W3): add 1 Doom.
}}

**Scene Flow.** Once the players are ashore, move to the Beastwood map.

\column

### Scene 2: The Beastwood
***The deer park. Night. ~30 min***

{{descriptive
Old oaks and thorn crowd together into walls, with grassy glades between them. A lantern hangs from a branch ahead, and another beyond it.
}}

**What's Happening.** The household hung lanterns to mark the way from the Landing to the Moon Mound (W6). Two watchers, the Pike brothers, stand in the South Hollow (W3) with a handbell.

**Lanterns.** Following the lanterns leads straight to the clearing. Leaving them means finding a way through the glades.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Survival DC 15.** Cross the Beastwood off the lantern line without losing the way. On a failure, the players come out at the wrong glade: add 1 Doom and 1 to the Crossing Track.

**Perception DC 14.** Hear the Pike brothers talking in the South Hollow before they see the players.
}}

**Scene Flow.** When the players reach the edge of the east clearing, run Scene 3.

\column

{{dm-sidebar
#### The Crossing Track
The ritual's clock. It starts at 0, 1 or 2 (Scene 1). On initiative count 10 of every round, add 1.

**0.** The lamps are lit. Hester begins the words.

**1.** Edie's pains come close together.

**2.** The lamplight turns cold and white. The air over the mound ripples like heat.

**3.** Every lantern in the Beastwood goes out.

**4.** A circle of black sky with no stars forms over the mound. Hester's arms lengthen: Many Hands has reach 30 ft. From now on, Ether Summons applies to every spell and curse cast within 60 ft. of the mound.

**5.** The child is coming. Hester's hands reach up toward the black circle.

**6.** The child is born, and the birth opens the door. Run *If the Door Opens*.

**Stopping the Track.** Before 6, the track stops when the last lamp goes out or when Hester dies. The black circle fades, and Edie's labour goes on without it.
}}

{{dm-sidebar
#### Doom
**Mythos Disadvantage.** Disadvantage on one roll.

**Murphy's Law.** A branch snaps underfoot, a lantern swings into view, a boot sinks in mud.

**Cut Off.** A fallen bough or a deep pool blocks the way. The players must go around.

**The Herd.** The park's deer bolt through the glade. Each creature in a 30-ft.-wide line makes a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) bludgeoning damage and Prone.

**Enemy Reinforcements.** A villager comes up the lantern line: an Awakened Cultist.

**Activate one Dread Ability.** Trigger a Dread Insight already held by a player.
}}

#### The Deer Park
Map: 10.01 in Foundry. One square is 2.5 ft.

**W1. The Landing.** A strip of trodden shore where the lake reaches into the park. The household's punt is drawn up on the mud.

**W2. The Lake Gap.** The path round the lake enters the wood here, by a stile in the park fence.

**W3. The South Hollow.** A low ring of earth. The Pike brothers, two **Awakened Cultists**, keep watch here with a handbell.

**W4. The Lantern Walk.** The glades north of the Landing. Lanterns hang from the branches every forty feet, leading east.

**W5. The East Pool.** Deep, cold water under the trees. Reeds at the edge.

**W6. The Moon Mound.** An oval mound in the east clearing, with the ritual on top: Edie on a pallet, **Hester Crayle** beside her, and five **Lamp Keepers** at five lamps on poles around the mound. The cart stands at the west edge of the clearing, with two **Corrupted Cultists**, the carters.

**W7. The Hart's Hollow.** A ring of earth in the north glades. The deer herd shelters here.

**W8. The Twin Pools.** Two small pools in the far north-west.

**W9. The Old Mound.** A low ring of earth in the northern glade. Empty.
}}

#### Running the Beastwood
The tree stands are terrain walls: they block movement, and a creature can see through the edge of one stand but not past it into the next glade. The glades are open ground.

### Scene 3: The Moon Mound
***The east clearing (W6). Night. ~60 min***

{{descriptive
An oval mound rises in the middle of the clearing. Five lamps on poles stand in a ring around it, and a woman lies on a pallet on top, under a blanket.
}}

**What's Happening.** Hester kneels at Edie's head and speaks the words. The Lamp Keepers face inward and chant with her. The carters wait by the cart. Edie is in labour and cannot move, run or fight.

**Who the Players See.** From the edge of the clearing, the woman on the mound is a pregnant woman with dark hair. Do not say whether she is Claudia. Hester's back is to them.

**Roll Initiative.** Hester is an Epic Boss: she acts on initiative count 10 and takes Responses after each player's turn. Add 1 to the Crossing Track on count 10 each round.

**The Lamps.** Each Lamp Keeper holds one lamp on a pole. A lamp goes out when its Keeper dies, or when a creature within 5 ft. of it uses an action to put it out. A lamp cannot be lit again tonight.

**Hester's Ward.** While two or more lamps burn, Hester has resistance to all damage.

**Edie.** Area spells and curses that cover the mound cover Edie. She has 9 HP and AC 10. At 0 HP she is dying and makes death saving throws; if she lives, the Birth (Scene 4) is made at Disadvantage.

### If the Door Opens
At 6, the child is born on the pallet, and the black circle over the mound opens into a hole. The **Greater Eye Fluke** drops through onto the mound beside Hester.

**One Round.** The fluke does not roll initiative. On the next initiative count 10 it crawls to Hester and merges with her. Every player gets a turn before then. If the fluke is dead by that count, the crossing fails: the door closes, and Hester fights on as she was.

**The Merge.** If the fluke reaches her, read:

{{descriptive
The eye climbs Hester and rips her own eye from its socket. Through the hole you can see her brain. The thing pushes in, and its tendrils dig into the brain. Hester stands up.
}}

The door closes. Replace Hester with **Hester Ascended**. She keeps the damage she has taken. The lamps no longer matter.

**Mother and Child.** Hester Ascended goes for Edie and the child first. The child has AC 10 and 1 HP. A player can pick the child up as part of their movement.

### Scene 4: The Birth
***The Moon Mound. Night. ~15 min***

{{descriptive
The lamps are out. The clearing is dark except for the moon, and Edie is crying for help.
}}

**What's Happening.** The ritual is broken but the labour is not over. Edie needs someone to deliver her child. If the track reached 6, the child is already born: skip the check.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Medicine DC 15.** One player delivers the child. Other players can help, giving Advantage.

- **Success.** A boy, small and loud. Mother and child are well.
- **Failure.** The child is well. Edie bleeds badly and lives only if the players get her to Silverbrook tonight.
}}

### Scene 5: The Bag
***The Moon Mound. Night. ~15 min***

{{descriptive
Hester's midwife's bag lies open by the pallet: scissors, twine, clean cloths, a bottle of brandy, and a bundle of folded papers.
}}

**What's Happening.** The papers are the two handouts: **The Proving**, in a hand that is not Hester's, and Hester's unfinished **Report to Mr. Connely**.

**The Reveal.** The woman on the mound is Edie Lamb, a dairymaid from Silverbrook. She is not Claudia. Edie knows Claudia: "Mrs. Bay. They kept us together in the cellar. They took her away in the carriage at dusk."

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Investigation DC 14.** The Proving was written by an educated hand, with a good pen, by someone used to giving orders. It is not the hand of anyone the players have met.
}}

### Resolution

**Outcome Ladder.**

- **Total success.** The track stopped before 6. Mother and child are well.
- **Mixed.** The door opened, but the players killed the fluke before it reached Hester.
- **Total failure.** The fluke merged with Hester. The Clairvoyant has her proof, whether the players killed Hester Ascended or fled.

**Rewards.** TBD.

**Threads and Dead Ends.**

- **Claudia.** Gone to town tonight in the closed carriage.
- **Mr. Connely.** The report was for him. Nothing says who he is.
- **The Proving.** "For ours" means another child. The players can guess whose. "Into me" means the writer wants this done to herself.
- **Hester's villagers.** Dead or gone. Silverbrook will have questions in the morning.

#### The Proving
*Found in Hester Crayle's bag. Two sheets of good paper, folded in four, in a neat, sloping hand.*

{{atbl-flavor
**The Proving.**

Take the girl down to the deer park when she is near her time. Begin at ten, when the moon is over the trees.

Five lamps on the five points, each kept by one of your own. Let none of them go out.

Lay her on the old mound, inside the lamps, head to the north.

When her pains come close together, speak the words on the second leaf, and do not stop.

The birth opens the door. Be under it when it does. What comes through will come to you. Do not fight it.

Write down everything: the hour, the moon, how long the door stays open, what comes, and what you feel as it takes you. If you cannot hold the pen, have one of yours write for you.

If the door opens for this one, it will open for ours. If it will cross into you, it will cross into me.

Send your account by the morning coach.
}}
