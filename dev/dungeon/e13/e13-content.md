# E13 Fear is the Key: episode content (source for the Foundry journal and the brew)

Level 17, 6 players, about 4 hours. One dungeon scene: 13.01 The Basement under Hawthorne Greens.

## Keeper summary
The secret stair from Episode Twelve ends in a basement no plan of Hawthorne Greens shows. Dr. Valentin Orme built it with the Clairvoyant's money: a treatment room where he injects eye flukes into his own orderlies, halls bent so badly that things from between the angles hunt in them, a vault where he fed a Saed until it grew, and, at the end, the Machine Ward, where a brass machine wired to seven sleepers drives the dream that cursed the players. Orme waits there with his watch and his dolls. In his study are the Clairvoyant's letters, signed by Lady Adela Blackmoor.

## Episode summary
- Players: 3 to 6, Level 17. Duration about 4 hours. Pacing: Crawl; Combat (Boss).
- Rooms: 8 (one scene).
- Cast: Dr. Valentin Orme; Fluke-Ridden Orderlies; Angle Hounds and the Elder Angle Hound; Gedwild; Porcelain Dolls.
- Reward: the end of the Damnation; the Clairvoyant's name. Treasure: TBD (John).
- References: Doom Counter; Curse System; E11 Damnation; E12 The Watch Stops.

## Timeline
- Six months ago: the Clairvoyant begins paying Dr. Orme through a private account.
- Four months ago: Orme takes delivery of a Saed in a sealed tank, sent by his patron, and begins feeding it.
- Two weeks ago: the machine is finished; the dream spell begins (Episode Eleven).
- Tonight: the players come down the stair (end of Episode Twelve).

## Episode structure
1. The Stair Foot and the Long Hall (13.01, 13.02). Exploration; the hall trap.
2. The Injection Ward (13.03). Combat: Fluke-Ridden Orderlies.
3. The Crooked Knot (13.04) and its halls. Combat: the Angle Hound pack.
4. Orme's Study (13.05). Reveal: the Clairvoyant is Lady Adela Blackmoor.
5. The Gas Works (13.06). Puzzle: start the fans to blow the gas out of the Machine Ward.
6. The Specimen Vault (13.07). Combat (Epic Boss): Gedwild.
7. The Machine Ward (13.08). Combat (Epic Boss): Dr. Orme and the machine.

Rooms 13.05 and 13.06 are off the Crooked Knot and can be visited in any order. The vault is the only way to the Machine Ward.

## Running the Basement
- Orme knows the players are down here. He does not come to meet them. If they rest, the Damnation from Episode Eleven comes back after a long rest while the machine runs; a short rest is safe.
- The Watch Stops: if Orme used it in Episode Twelve, he has it again tonight (a day has passed). He uses it once, when he is first Bloodied.
- The gas: if the players worked out in Episode Twelve that Orme's mesmerism is a gas, give them Advantage on the first Mesmeric Gas save of the fight. The fans in the Gas Works blow the gas out of the Machine Ward.
- Doom: the machine spends Doom in 13.08 (see The Machine).

## 13.01 The Stair Foot
Read-aloud: The stair ends in a brick landing that smells of ether and wet stone. Crates are stacked against the wall. An orderly's white jacket hangs on a coat rack, the left sleeve stiff with something dried. A single door leads east.

How it plays: Nothing here attacks. The jacket's pocket holds a brass key tag stamped "W" (the Machine Ward door, see 13.08) and a used injector with a few drops of cloudy fluid in it.
- Medicine DC 17: the fluid holds something alive and very small; it moves toward light.

Resolution: the players go east into the Long Hall.

## 13.02 The Long Hall
Read-aloud: A service tunnel runs east, longer than the house above it. Iron pipes sweat along the wall, a gutter of black water runs beside the flags, and the tunnel bends south at an angle no builder would choose. A wheelchair stands in the middle of the floor facing the way you came.

How it plays: the hall trap is in the bend.
- Trap, The Sleep Line (hall kind): a tripwire across the 15 ft of hall just past the bend opens a gas cock in the pipes. Each creature in the bend makes a DC 19 Constitution save: failure, 66 (12d10) poison damage and Poisoned until the end of its next turn; success, half damage only. Spot: Perception DC 18 (the pipe at the bend has a fresh brass valve; the flags are scuffed in a line). Disarm: Thieves' Tools DC 18, or close the valve by hand (Athletics DC 16) before stepping in.

Resolution: the hall ends at the Injection Ward door.

## 13.03 The Injection Ward
Read-aloud: White tile, a drain in the middle of the floor and six iron chairs with leather straps. Along the far wall a steel counter holds racks of injectors and jars of small pale eyes turning slowly in cloudy fluid. Six orderlies in white stand by the chairs, still, as if waiting for patients. Each one has a wet pale eye in its left socket that is not his own, and each one turns to look at you.

How it plays: Combat. Six Fluke-Ridden Orderlies. Orme injected fluke larvae into his own staff; the flukes ride them. When an orderly drops, the larva in its eye crawls out and goes for the nearest open eye. Kill order matters: an orderly killed next to a friend puts a larva next to that friend.
- The counter: the jars hold more larvae. A creature that breaks or opens a jar releases one Eye Fluke Larva (Init 20). A player can burn the counter (any fire damage of 10 or more): every jar dies.
- Medicine DC 17 on a fallen orderly: the man is alive under the fluke. Cutting the larva out (Medicine DC 19, 1 minute) saves him.

Resolution: when the orderlies and larvae are down, the passage south opens toward the Crooked Knot. A saved orderly (Ned Pell, Tom Quill, or any of the six) knows only that "the doctor keeps the big room at the end, past the jars".

## 13.04 The Crooked Knot
Read-aloud: Four halls meet in a small room that has no square corner anywhere. A broken mirror lies on the floor. The walls lean. In the corners, where they meet at angles too sharp to stand in, the air is thin and blue, like the edge of a flame.

How it plays: Combat. Five Angle Hounds and the Elder Angle Hound. They live in the angles: the Knot and its four halls have dozens of sharp corners, and the hounds step from one to another. They come through the corners when the players reach the middle of the Knot, and they chase anywhere in the halls.
- Sealing a corner: magical darkness in a corner, or a creature standing in it, seals it. Salt, chalk or plaster pressed into the angle (an action) rounds it off and seals it too.
- Kill the Elder first and the pack scatters: when the Elder dies, each remaining hound makes a DC 15 Wisdom save or flees through the nearest corner and does not return.

Resolution: the Knot opens north to Orme's Study, west to the Gas Works and south toward the Specimen Vault.

## 13.05 Orme's Study
Read-aloud: A small study with a worn rug, a mahogany desk under a green lamp, shelves of medical books and a cot made up with hospital corners. On a shelf above the desk sit a row of porcelain dolls in lace, every face turned toward the chair.

How it plays: a reveal. The desk holds Orme's ledgers and a bundle of letters tied with violet ribbon.
- The ledgers: payments every month for six months from "Lady A. B., by private account". One entry reads "Specimen received. Feeding begun."
- The letters (Handout: The Patron's Letters): in a beautiful, practised hand, the same hand as the letter from the midwife's bag in Episode Ten. The last letter is sealed in violet wax with a crest of a black moor-hen, and it is signed "Adela Blackmoor".
- History DC 15: the Blackmoor crest; Lord and Lady Blackmoor are among the oldest families in North Ward.
- Insight DC 15 (comparing the hand to the Episode Ten letter): the same writer, without doubt.
- The dolls: each has a name card in Orme's hand, the name of one of the seven patients "removed below". Five dolls have blank cards. Orme talks to them.

Resolution: the players know who the Clairvoyant is.

## 13.06 The Gas Works
Read-aloud: Four round steel tanks squat in a concrete room, joined by a knot of copper pipe. Against the north wall a fan the height of a man sits in an iron housing, still. A workbench holds rubber gas masks and gauges whose needles all point to red.

How it plays: a puzzle. This plant makes Orme's gas and feeds it through ducts into the Machine Ward. The fan blows down the same ducts.
- Reading the gauges (Investigation DC 18 or Tinker's Tools): tank three is over pressure; venting it before starting the fan is safe.
- Starting the fan: the lever is seized (Athletics DC 20), or a Tinker's Tools check DC 18 frees it. While the fan runs, Orme's Mesmeric Gas and the machine's Gas Vent do nothing in the Machine Ward.
- Hazard, Tank Three: any fire, lightning or a spark (a failed Tinker's Tools check by 5 or more) before tank three is vented: the tank bursts. Each creature in the room makes a DC 19 Dexterity save: failure, 55 (10d10) fire damage; success, half. The fan housing is wrecked and cannot be started.
- The gas masks: six rubber masks. A creature wearing one has Advantage on saves against Mesmeric Gas.

Resolution: the fan runs, or the players leave it.

## 13.07 The Specimen Vault
Read-aloud: Shelves of great glass jars line the walls, each holding a pale brain in green fluid. In the middle of the floor a glass tank as big as a carriage lies broken open. Something has been eating from the jars. It is still here, a brain the size of a bear on a nest of grey tendrils and spindly legs, and as you come in it turns toward you without a face.

How it plays: Combat (Epic Boss). Gedwild, the last form of the Saed the Clairvoyant sent Orme. It feeds on minds.
- The jars: eight jars on the shelves (AC 12, HP 5 each). At the start of each Init 10 turn, Gedwild eats one jar within its reach and regains 45 HP. Breaking the jars denies it. A thrown flask of oil or any fire damage of 10 or more on a shelf breaks the two jars on it.
- The door to the Vault Passage is barred from the vault side; Gedwild is between the players and the bar.

Resolution: when Gedwild dies, its brain splits and three small Saeds scuttle into the jars' fluid and die. The Vault Passage leads to the Machine Ward.

## 13.08 The Machine Ward
Read-aloud: A tiled ward under a vaulted roof. In the middle stands a brass machine the size of a cottage: gears, glass tubes and a great clock face whose pendulum swings with no sound. Black cables run from it across the floor to twelve iron beds set around it like the numbers on a dial. Seven beds hold sleepers under white sheets. Five are empty, made up with fresh linen, waiting. On the end of every bed sits a porcelain doll. Dr. Valentin Orme stands at the clock with his watch in his hand. "You came all this way to wake up," he says. "Everyone does, in the end."

How it plays: Combat (Epic Boss). Dr. Orme and the machine.
- The double door is locked; the "W" key tag from 13.01 opens it, or Thieves' Tools DC 20.
- The machine: while it runs, Orme regains HP on his turn (Machine-Fed) and the GM may spend Doom on The Machine's effects. Stopping it ends the Damnation for every player character.
- Stopping the machine: destroy it (AC 19, HP 250, immune to poison and psychic damage), or shut it down at the clock face: three successful DC 20 checks (Arcana, Tinker's Tools, or Medicine to free the sleepers safely), each an action. Destroying it while sleepers are still wired in wakes them screaming; each sleeper still wired takes 10 (3d6) psychic damage and survives.
- The sleepers: the seven patients marked "removed below" in Orme's case notes (Episode Twelve), alive. They wake when the machine stops. The five empty beds are for the players; their dolls' name cards are blank.
- If the fans in 13.06 are running, Mesmeric Gas and Gas Vent do nothing.

### The Machine (Doom effects)
The GM spends Doom on Orme's Init 10 turn, once per round.
- Lullaby (1 Doom). The pendulum swings and every creature in the ward hears a lullaby. Each player character makes a DC 20 Wisdom save: failure, the creature falls asleep (Unconscious) until it takes damage or another creature uses an action to wake it.
- Gas Vent (1 Doom). A 20-ft-radius sphere around one gas cylinder fills with Orme's gas. The area is Heavily Obscured until the end of the next round, and creatures inside have Disadvantage on Mesmeric Gas saves. (No effect while the fans run.)
- The Dolls Wake (2 Doom). Four Porcelain Dolls climb down from the beds. They roll initiative.
- A Sleeper's Nightmare (2 Doom). One sleeper's nightmare steps out of its bed: place a Vision (Episode Eleven stat block) beside the bed. It rolls initiative.
- The Clock Strikes (3 Doom). Orme's Show Me Your Fear recharges and he regains 52 HP.

Resolution: when Orme dies or the machine stops and Orme falls, the sleepers wake, the Damnation lifts, and the dolls go still. If the machine stops while Orme lives, he loses Machine-Fed and fights on.

### Resolution
- Outcome: the dream spell ends. The players carry the Clairvoyant's name out of the basement.
- Rewards: TBD (John). Story items: the Patron's Letters; Orme's gold watch (it stops when he dies).
- Threads: Lady Adela Blackmoor (Episode Fourteen); the Saed she sent Orme (where did she get it?); the seven sleepers, who saw her in their dreams.

## Handout: The Patron's Letters
"Doctor. Your last report pleased me. The dreamers you have chosen are strong, and the dream holds. Feed the specimen as I showed you; it must be grown before the spring. Our adversaries have come close twice now. If they find their way to you, put them to sleep with the others. I will not be disappointed again. A.B."

The last letter, sealed in violet wax: "Valentin. No more delays. The girl is near her time and I will need you at the end. Burn these. Adela Blackmoor."

## Stat blocks

### Fluke-Ridden Orderly
Medium Humanoid, Neutral Evil
AC 17. Initiative +2 (12). HP 93 (17d8 + 17). Speed 30 ft.
STR 18 (+4) DEX 14 (+2) CON 13 (+1) INT 6 (-2) WIS 12 (+1) CHA 6 (-2)
Condition Immunities Charmed, Frightened. Senses Passive Perception 11. Languages understands Common but can't speak. CR 13 (XP 10,000; PB +5)
Traits
Disposition: Grappler. The orderly moves to the nearest creature, grabs it and injects it. It drags grabbed creatures toward the other orderlies.
Ridden. When the orderly drops to 0 Hit Points, an Eye Fluke Larva crawls out of its eye into its space. The larva rolls initiative.
Actions
Restraining Grip. Melee Attack Roll: +17, reach 5 ft. Hit: 28 (5d8 + 6) bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 18).
Larva Injection (Recharge 5-6). Constitution Saving Throw: DC 19, one creature the orderly is grappling. Failure: 56 (10d10 + 1) poison damage, and the target has the Blinded condition until the end of its next turn and gains a Tormenting Pox. Success: Half damage only.
Bonus Actions
Drag. The orderly moves up to half its Speed, pulling a creature it is grappling with it.
Reactions
Pin. Trigger: A creature the orderly is grappling fails an escape check. Response: The creature has the Prone condition.

### Eye Fluke Larva
Tiny Aberration, Unaligned
AC 16. Initiative +4 (14). HP 19 (7d4 + 2). Speed 20 ft., climb 20 ft.
STR 2 (-4) DEX 18 (+4) CON 12 (+1) INT 3 (-4) WIS 12 (+1) CHA 3 (-4)
Senses Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 11. CR 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
Traits
Disposition: Seeker. The larva goes for the nearest creature whose eyes are open.
Eyeless Prey. The larva can't use Leap for the Eye against a creature that has the Blinded condition or has its eyes shut.
Actions
Leap for the Eye. Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 19, one creature within 10 feet. Failure: 14 (4d6) psychic damage, the target has the Blinded condition until the end of its next turn and gains a Tormenting Pox, and the larva dies inside the eye. STR 16 (+3) DEX 20 (+5) CON 15 (+2) INT 7 (-2) WIS 16 (+3) CHA 10 (+0)
Condition Immunities Incapacitated. Senses Blindsight 30 ft., Passive Perception 13. Languages understands Deep Speech. CR 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)
Traits
Disposition: Skirmisher. The hound bites and steps back into a corner. It goes for a creature standing away from its friends.
Pack Hunter. The hound has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if another hound is within 5 feet of that creature.
Corner Walker. The hound can move from one corner (a place where two walls meet at an angle) to another corner within 60 feet that it can see, using 10 feet of movement. A corner with magical darkness, a creature, or salt, chalk or plaster pressed into it is sealed, and the hound can't use it.

Bonus Actions
Back Into the Angle. The hound moves into a corner within 5 feet and has the Invisible condition until it makes an attack roll.
