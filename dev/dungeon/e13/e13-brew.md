{{atbl-keeper-summary
##### Down the Stair
The secret stair under Hawthorne Greens ends in a basement no plan of the house shows. Dr. Orme built it with the Clairvoyant's money: a ward where he injects eye flukes into his own orderlies, halls bent so badly that hounds from between the angles hunt in them, and a vault where he fed a Saed until it grew. At the end, in the Machine Ward, a brass machine wired to sleepers drives the dream that cursed the players. Orme waits there with his watch and his dolls. In his study are the Clairvoyant's letters, signed by Lady Adela Blackmoor.
}}

### Timeline
{{noIndent
**Six months ago.** The Clairvoyant began paying Dr. Orme through a private account.

**Four months ago.** Orme took delivery of a Saed in a sealed tank, sent by his patron, and began to feed it.

**Two weeks ago.** The machine was finished. The dream began (Episode Eleven).

**Tonight.** The players come down the stair (end of Episode Twelve).
}}

\column

### Episode Structure
One map, 13.01 The Basement. The stair foot leads down the Long Hall to the Injection Ward (fight), then the Crooked Knot (fight). From the Knot, Orme's Study (the reveal) and the Gas Works (a puzzle) branch off. The south hall leads to the Specimen Vault (Epic Boss), the only way to the Machine Ward (Epic Boss).

Each room has a map note in Foundry that opens its page in the journal "E13 Fear is the Key".

\column

### Running the Basement
**Orme Knows.** He knows the players are below. He does not come to meet them.

**Resting.** While the machine runs, the Damnation comes back after a long rest. A short rest is safe.

**The Watch Stops.** Orme has it again tonight. He uses it once, the first time he is Bloodied.

**The Gas.** If the players worked out in Episode Twelve that Orme's mesmerism is a gas, give them Advantage on their first Mesmeric Gas save. The fans in the Gas Works blow the gas out of the Machine Ward.

**No Free Healing.** Solving a room never restores Hit Points.

### Scene 1: The Stair Foot
***13.01. Night. ~5 min***

{{descriptive
The stair ends in a brick landing that smells of ether and wet stone. An orderly's white jacket hangs on a coat rack, one sleeve stiff with something dried.
}}

The jacket's pocket holds a brass key tag stamped "W" (the Machine Ward door) and a used injector with a few drops of cloudy fluid.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Medicine DC 17.** The fluid holds something alive and very small. It moves toward light.
}}

### Scene 2: The Long Hall
***13.02. Night. ~10 min***

{{descriptive
A service tunnel runs east, longer than the house above it. Pipes sweat along the wall, and the tunnel bends south at an angle no builder would choose. A wheelchair faces the way you came.
}}

**The Sleep Line.** A tripwire across the 15 ft. of hall just past the bend opens a gas cock in the pipes. Each creature in the bend makes a DC 19 Constitution save: 66 (12d10) poison damage and Poisoned until the end of its next turn on a failure, half on a success.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Perception DC 18.** A fresh brass valve on the pipe at the bend; the flags are scuffed in a line.

**Thieves' Tools DC 18** disarms the wire; **Athletics DC 16** closes the valve by hand.
}}


### Scene 3: The Injection Ward
***13.03. Night. ~40 min***

{{descriptive
White tile, a drain in the floor and six iron chairs with leather straps. Jars of small pale eyes turn slowly on the counter. Six orderlies stand by the chairs, each with a wet pale eye in his left socket that is not his own, and each turns to look at you.
}}

Six **Fluke-Ridden Orderlies**. Orme injected fluke larvae into his own staff; the flukes ride them. When an orderly drops, an **Eye Fluke Larva** crawls out of his eye and leaps for the nearest open eye. Kill order matters.

**The Counter.** Breaking a jar releases a larva (Init 20). Fire of 10 or more on the counter kills every jar.

**The Men Inside.** Each orderly is alive under the fluke. Cutting the larva out of a fallen one (Medicine DC 19, 1 minute) saves him. He knows only that "the doctor keeps the big room at the end, past the jars".

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Medicine DC 17.** The fallen orderly is still breathing.
}}


### Scene 4: The Crooked Knot
***13.04. Night. ~45 min***

{{descriptive
Four halls meet in a small room with no square corner anywhere. A broken mirror lies on the floor. In the corners, where the walls meet too sharply to stand in, the air is thin and blue.
}}

Five **Angle Hounds** and the **Elder Angle Hound** come through the corners when the players reach the middle of the Knot, and chase them anywhere in its halls. They step from corner to corner.

**Sealing a Corner.** Magical darkness, a creature standing in it, or salt, chalk or plaster pressed into the angle (an action) seals a corner.

**Head of the Pack.** When the Elder dies, each hound makes a DC 15 Wisdom save or leaves through the nearest corner.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Arcana DC 17.** The hounds can't use a corner that is filled or dark.
}}


### Scene 5: Orme's Study
***13.05. Night. ~20 min***

{{descriptive
A small study: a worn rug, a mahogany desk under a green lamp, a cot made up with hospital corners. On a shelf sit porcelain dolls in lace, every face turned toward the chair.
}}

**The Ledgers.** Monthly payments for six months from "Lady A. B., by private account". One entry: "Specimen received. Feeding begun."

**The Letters.** A bundle tied with violet ribbon (Handouts), in the same beautiful hand as the letter from the midwife's bag in Episode Ten. The last, sealed in violet wax with a crest of a black moor-hen, is signed **Adela Blackmoor**. The players now know who the Clairvoyant is.

**The Dolls.** Each has a name card in Orme's hand: the seven patients "removed below". Five more cards are blank.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**History DC 15.** The Blackmoor crest; one of the oldest families in North Ward.

**Insight DC 15.** Laid beside the Episode Ten letter: the same writer, without doubt.
}}


### Scene 6: The Gas Works
***13.06. Night. ~20 min***

{{descriptive
Four round steel tanks squat in a concrete room, joined by copper pipe. A fan the height of a man sits still in an iron housing. The gauges on the bench all point to red.
}}

This plant makes Orme's gas and feeds it through ducts into the Machine Ward. The fan blows down the same ducts.

**Starting the Fan.** The lever is seized: Athletics DC 20, or Tinker's Tools DC 18. While it runs, Mesmeric Gas and Gas Vent do nothing in the Machine Ward.

**Tank Three.** Fire, lightning or a spark (a Tinker's Tools check failed by 5 or more) before tank three is vented: it bursts. DC 19 Dexterity save, 55 (10d10) fire, half on a success. The fan is wrecked.

**Gas Masks.** Six on the bench: Advantage on Mesmeric Gas saves.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Investigation DC 18 or Tinker's Tools.** Tank three is over pressure; vent it before starting the fan.
}}

### Scene 7: The Specimen Vault
***13.07. Night. ~50 min***

{{descriptive
Shelves of great glass jars hold pale brains in green fluid. A glass tank the size of a carriage lies broken open. Something the size of a bear, a brain on a nest of tendrils and spindly legs, turns toward you without a face.
}}

**Gedwild** (Epic Boss), the last form of the Saed the Clairvoyant sent Orme. It feeds on minds and goes for spellcasters.

**The Jars.** Eight jars (AC 12, HP 5). At the start of each Init 10 turn Gedwild eats one within 15 ft. and regains 45 HP. Fire of 10 or more on a shelf breaks its two jars.

**After.** Its brain splits; three small Saeds scuttle into the spilled fluid and die.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Arcana DC 18.** It is eating the jars to heal. Break them.
}}


### Scene 8: The Machine Ward
***13.08. Night. ~70 min***

{{descriptive
A brass machine the size of a cottage, its great clock face swinging a silent pendulum. Cables run to twelve iron beds around it like the numbers on a dial. Seven hold sleepers. Five are empty, made up, waiting. A porcelain doll sits on every bed. Dr. Orme stands at the clock with his watch in his hand. "You came all this way to wake up," he says. "Everyone does, in the end."
}}

**Dr. Valentin Orme** (Epic Boss) and the machine. While it runs, Orme is Machine-Fed and the GM may spend Doom on it. Stopping it ends the Damnation.

**Stopping the Machine.** Destroy it (AC 19, HP 250, immune to poison and psychic), or three DC 20 checks at the clock face (Arcana, Tinker's Tools, or Medicine to free the sleepers safely), one action each. Destroyed with sleepers wired in, each takes 10 (3d6) psychic and lives.

**The Sleepers.** The seven patients "removed below" (Episode Twelve). They wake when the machine stops.


\column

#### The Machine (Doom)
Spend on Orme's Init 10 turn, once a round.

| Doom | Effect |
|:--|:--|
| 1 | **Lullaby.** DC 20 Wis, each player: asleep until damaged or woken (an action). |
| 1 | **Gas Vent.** 20-ft. sphere at a gas cylinder: Heavily Obscured, Disadvantage on Mesmeric Gas saves. None while the fans run. |
| 2 | **The Dolls Wake.** Four Porcelain Dolls climb down. |
| 2 | **A Sleeper's Nightmare.** A Vision (Episode Eleven) steps out beside a bed. |
| 3 | **The Clock Strikes.** Show Me Your Fear recharges; Orme regains 52 HP. |


\column

### Resolution
**Outcome.** The dream spell ends and the Damnation lifts. The players carry the Clairvoyant's name out of the basement.

**Rewards.** TBD. Story items: The Patron's Letters; Orme's gold watch (it stops when he dies).

**Threads and Dead Ends.**

- **Lady Adela Blackmoor.** The Clairvoyant, by her own hand (Episode Fourteen).
- **The Saed.** Where did she get it?
- **The seven sleepers.** They saw her in their dreams.
