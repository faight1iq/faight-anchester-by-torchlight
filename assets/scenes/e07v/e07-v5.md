{{atbl-keeper-summary
##### No Shadow Anywhere
The Clarion Society holds Dr. Aldous Pemberton in a small white room ringed by lamps, so he casts no shadow. His shadow is the Smiling Man, and it can leave him only while he sleeps. The party questions the doctor and the people around him: Evelyn Ridgely, Sergeant Bay, President Byrgenwright, and Hazel Attwater of The Worker. Every answer points to the Lord of Light's ball. Then Bryony Thatch finds a way to end the Smiling Man: a ritual from the Pale Journal pages that splits the light in a man from the dark. For it to work, Pemberton has to sleep.
}}

### Timeline
{{noIndent
**Fifteen years ago.** A collector had the Pale Journals stolen from the Temple of Anhur in Sai Abbadon.

**Six months ago.** The Special Police began building super-soldiers from a serum found in one of the Journals.

**Weeks ago.** At Paschendale the serum turned the volunteers into monsters. The city was told it was a victory.

**Days ago.** The players saved Freitman Bay, freed Evelyn Ridgely, and captured Dr. Pemberton. Colonel Standard took Bay's wife, Claudia, to the Clairvoyant.

**Last night.** The Clarion Society shut Pemberton in a white room under the lamps. The players have kept him awake since.

**Today.** The Worker is passing from hand to hand. Pemberton is very tired.
}}

\column

### Episode Structure
Almost all of this episode takes place in the Clarion Society clubhouse. There is no trail to follow; the players gather answers from the people in the building and from Hazel Attwater at The Worker's press.

Four sources each hold one piece. Together they put Colonel Standard and the Clairvoyant at the Lord of Light's ball. That is the reason to go, and getting in is Episode Eight's problem.

The episode ends with the ritual. Bryony Thatch and President Byrgenwright cast it; the players feed the circle and fight the Smiling Man through a rift into the Shadowfell.

**Add 1 Doom** for every hour of play Pemberton is kept awake, and every time a player tests the lamps or the straps.

### Scene 1: The White Room
***The Clarion Society, Ash Ward. Day. ~60 min***

{{descriptive
A small room painted white from floor to ceiling. Brass lamps on iron stands ring a wooden chair in the middle. The man strapped into it casts no shadow.
}}

**What's Happening.** Dr. Pemberton sits in the chair, awake and exhausted. A Clarion Society member keeps watch at the door. Evelyn Ridgely and Sergeant Freitman Bay are in the clubhouse. President Byrgenwright wanders from room to room and can turn up anywhere, this room included.

**The Worker.** If the players did not pick up The Worker in Episode Six ("The Clairvoyant Behind the Experiments," Episode Six handouts), it is in the clubhouse today. Hand it out. Its byline, Hazel Attwater, is the lead out of the building.

**Clues.** Each source knows one thing. See Who Knows What.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Insight DC 14.** Pemberton is telling the truth: he does not know where Standard reports or how he travels.

**Medicine DC 12.** Pemberton has not slept in more than a day. He will not last another.

**Investigation DC 10.** The lamps leave no shadow anywhere in the room, not even under the chair.
}}

\column

#### Who Knows What

| Source | Gives the Players |
|:--|:--|
| Pemberton | Standard works for someone. |
| Evelyn | Standard is picking up his dress uniform for the ball. |
| Byrgenwright | Someone high in the government has been secretly tracking the Pale Journal page. |
| Hazel Attwater | Her source thinks the Clairvoyant will be at the ball. |

**Sergeant Bay.** He does not know that Standard left the safehouse with Claudia. Telling him is the players' choice.

**President Byrgenwright.** He gives his piece in fragments and non sequiturs: a ministry stamp, a name crossed out on a library slip, "they asked after the page twice." Let the players put it together. Do not explain it for them.

{{dm-sidebar
#### Doom
**Mythos Disadvantage.** Disadvantage on one roll.

**Murphy's Law.** A strap gives, a lamp needs trimming, a door sticks.

**A Shadow on the Wall.** For one moment someone sees a grin in a corner where no shadow should be. Wisdom save or Frightened until the end of the scene.

**The Wrong Fragment.** Byrgenwright says something true about a different matter, and a player believes it is about this one.

**Activate one Dread Ability.** Trigger a Dread Insight already held by a player.
}}

\column

### Scene 2: Hazel Attwater
***The Worker's press, Meskawaki University. Day. ~30 min***

**What's Happening.** Hazel prints The Worker in a cellar under the university (Foundry 06.14). She trusts no one sent by money or by the state.

**Clues.** Her source thinks the Clairvoyant will be at the ball. She will not say who the source is.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Persuasion DC 15.** Hazel tells what her source said. On a failure, she wants something first: a story she can print.

**Insight DC 14.** She is protecting the source, not lying.
}}

**Scene Flow.** When the players have the pieces, or the Counter reaches 12, run Scene 3.

### Scene 3: Building the Ritual
***The Clarion Society. ~30 min***

**What's Happening.** Bryony Thatch has been reading the Pale Journal pages the party has found. She asks one player to work through them with her. Together they put together a ritual that separates the light and dark parts of a person so the soul can be healed. Bryony cannot cast it alone. She asks President Byrgenwright to cast it with her.

**The Price.** The ritual needs Pemberton asleep. The players must let him sleep, in the ritual room, inside the circle.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Arcana DC 16.** The player and Bryony read the ritual right. On a failure, it still works, but the first blast in Scene 4 does nothing.

**Persuasion DC 12.** Byrgenwright agrees. He drifts off the subject twice before he does.
}}

#### Reading the Map
The ritual room is a white stone chamber in the Clarion Society. When the ritual begins, its back wall opens onto the Shadowfell, a broken land of rock and chasm that runs off the edge of the map. Map: 07.01 Out of the Shadows in Foundry.


{{noIndent
**1. The altar.**

**2. Bryony's station.**

**3. Byrgenwright's station.**

**4. The circle.** Pemberton sleeps at its heart.
}}


{{noIndent
**5. The circle's points.** Four of them: top, bottom, left, right. The players stand here to feed the ritual.

**6. The broken wall.** The opening into the Shadowfell.

**7. The Shadowfell.** Where the Smiling Man hides.
}}

### Scene 4: Out of the Shadows
***The ritual room. Night. ~60 min***

{{descriptive
The circle burns red, gold, and blue on the white floor. Above the sleeping doctor hangs a pale light the size of a man. Behind it, the back wall is gone.
}}

**What's Happening.** As Pemberton falls asleep, the ritual begins. A floating light, a ghost of the man he was, rises above him. The back wall of the chamber opens onto the Shadowfell. The light speaks: it can feel the shadow hiding in there. Roll initiative.

**The Circle.** The players stand at the circle's points and feed it. Bryony and Byrgenwright cast from their stations. The ritual protects them both; nothing in the fight can harm them.

**The Blasts.** On Bryony's turn and on Byrgenwright's turn, the light fires a blast of radiant energy into the Shadowfell. It shows the players where the Smiling Man is, and it weakens him. Choose one effect for each blast.

| Blast | Effect |
|:-:|:--|
| Exposed | His AC is lowered until his next turn. |
| Opened | Player hits are critical hits until his next turn. |
| Burned | Player hits deal double damage until his next turn. |
| Dimmed | His hits deal half damage until his next turn. |
| Released | End Paralyzed on every player. |


**The Smiling Man.** He fights from the dark. In dim light or darkness, Peel Away makes attacks against him use AC 30. The blasts are what let the players reach him; they are the way to win, not a race to drain 1,080 hit points.

\column

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Perception DC 15.** Find the Smiling Man in the Shadowfell without a blast. On a success, the player may attack him this turn.

}}

**Scene Flow.** When the Smiling Man falls or flees, the rift closes and the light sinks back into the doctor.


### Resolution

**Outcome Ladder.**

- **Total success.** The Smiling Man falls. Pemberton wakes, and his shadow is only a shadow.
- **Mixed.** The Smiling Man escapes with Bag Trick before the rift closes.
- **Total failure.** He takes a player's memories with Excise and is gone into the dark.
- **Pyrrhic.** He falls, and a player stays Paralyzed in the Shadowfell as it closes.

**Rewards.** The players leave with a reason to attend the ball: the man who took Claudia and the one he answers to will both be there.

**Threads and Dead Ends.**

- **The ball.** Episode Eight.
- **Claudia.** With the Clairvoyant until the child is born.
- **Hazel's source.** Unnamed.

{{castMember
##### The Smiling Man
*Medium aberration, CR 13. Dr. Pemberton's shadow.*

AC25; HP1080; Speed 40, fly 40; Scalpel+16/10d8+10; Excise DC20 Int/20d8+20; The Weeping Smile DC20 Cha
{stats}

*Description*: A bowler hat, round spectacles weeping, a wide white grin. Nothing else can be seen.

*Mannerisms*: Peel Away in the dark. Bag Trick to vanish.

*Intent*: Stay hidden in the Shadowfell and return to the doctor.
}}


**Traits.** *Only the Smile:* identifying him later has disadvantage. *Shadow Form:* moves through a 1-inch gap; advantage on Stealth in dim light or darkness. *Sundered by Sunlight:* disadvantage on everything in direct sunlight. *Legendary Resistance* (3/day).

**Actions.** *Scalpel* +16, 10d8+10 slashing; a Humanoid at half HP or less starts bleeding out, 3d6 each turn until treated. *Excise* only against a Humanoid at half HP or less: DC 20 Int or 20d8+20 slashing, and he removes a memory, a spell, a skill, or a language. *The Weeping Smile:* DC 20 Cha or Paralyzed for 1 minute (Stunned on a success). *Bag Trick:* he climbs into his bag and is gone.

**Reaction.** *Peel Away:* attacked in dim light or darkness, he flattens against a surface; the attack uses AC 30, and on a miss he slides 20 ft.
