##### Held for Questioning
After the Skohle reading ends in murder, the Watch brings the survivors to North Harbor Police Headquarters to answer questions. Detective Hawkins wants to know what they saw in the dark. Down in the cells sits Benjamin Payne, bagman for Wystan Aldous of Brasshold. Merewyn Osgood means to answer Aldous in the only language he understands, and her gunmen are coming through a side door a bought sergeant left on the latch. In the morgue below lies the Smiling Man's latest victim, a bluestocking who asked too many questions. She will not stay on the slab.
}}

### Timeline
{{noIndent
**Four months ago.** Dr. Aldous Pemberton attends a reading of a Pale Journal page above a tobacconist on Lantern Avenue. He is changed by it, and his shadow begins to hunt.

**Two months ago.** The first body is found in the lower wards, the skull opened above the eyebrows. The lower wards name the killer the Smiling Man.

**Three days ago.** Miss Prudence Quill, a bluestocking who had been asking questions above her station, is found dead in her rooms. She lies in the North Harbor morgue.

**Last night.** Sergeant Knight books Benjamin Payne into the cells on a minor charge, on Merewyn Osgood's coin.

**Tonight.** Claude Skohle's reading ends in murder. The Watch brings in everyone left standing.

**Third bell.** Merewyn's gunmen come through the side door.
}}

\column

### Episode Structure
A police station under siege, then a morgue in the dark. The players are witnesses, not prisoners. Detective Hawkins questions them one at a time in front of his Murder Board, the station's whole case against the Smiling Man. What they read there carries them into Episode Two.

At the third bell, Merewyn Osgood's gunmen come through the side door to kill Payne and send Brasshold a message. The players fight them off, but fire and gunfire drive them down the stair into the morgue. There the Blue Stocking, Miss Quill, rises as the Dancing Spinster. She is far beyond a level four party. Getting out alive is the win.

#### Map Locations
**1. Vestibule.** Double doors to Harbor Street. A constable dozes on the bench by the door.

**2. Duty Hall.** Benches, pillars, and the sergeant's desk on its raised platform. Sergeant Knight keeps the blotter here.

**3. Captain's Office.** Parquet floor, a red desk, a wall safe. Captain Till keeps the door shut and the Osgood writ in his drawer.

**4. Evidence Room.** Shelves of tagged boxes and a locked wire cage. Payne's effects sit in the cage.

**5. Detectives' Office.** A rug, a long table, and the Murder Board on the wall. Hawkins questions the players here.

**6. Muster Room.** Benches where the constables take orders. Tonight the witnesses wait here.

\column

**7. Bunk Room.** Cots for the night watch. Two constables sleep in their boots.

**8. Armory.** Racks of truncheons, service revolvers, a riot shotgun, and one Tommy gun in a locked case.

**9. Storeroom.** Crates of files and lamp oil. The side door opens onto the alley. Knight leaves it off the latch at the third bell.

**10. Stair.** Stone steps down to the morgue.

**11. Turnkey's Desk.** The cell log and the keys, at the head of the cell block.

**12. Cell Block.** Three corridors of barred cells. Payne is in the far corridor, alone.

#### Weapons
The players came as witnesses, so they keep what they carried in. When the shooting starts, the Armory (8) holds a riot shotgun, a Tommy gun, service revolvers and truncheons. The ammunition is in the Storeroom (9).

#### The Blue Stocking
Miss Prudence Quill read in the Meskawaki University library at night and asked the librarians about the Pale Journals. The Smiling Man took what she knew three days ago. Something of the shadow stayed in her. When the noise of the fight reaches the morgue, she gets up.

#### Running the Questioning
Hawkins takes the players one at a time. While one is questioned, the others wait in the Muster Room (6) with a single constable. They can talk, listen at doors, and walk as far as the Duty Hall (2) before someone asks where they are going. Each player can attempt one bit of mischief while they wait: read the blotter, chat up the turnkey, look into the Evidence Room. Add 1 to the Doom Counter for each one that is noticed.

### Scene 1: Answering Questions
***Duty Hall (2) and Muster Room (6), 1 AM. ~30 min***

{{descriptive
Gas lamps hiss above rows of oak benches. A sergeant's desk stands on a raised platform at the end of the hall, and beyond it a barred door leads to the cells.
}}

**What's Happening.** The Watch has brought every survivor of the Skohle reading here as witnesses. No one is under arrest. Sergeant Dewey Knight takes their names at the desk and seats them in the Muster Room to wait for Detective Hawkins. Captain Till's door stays shut. Knight checks his watch against the hall clock more than a man on a quiet shift should.

**Clues.** The Blotter Page lies open on Knight's desk. A DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) or Charisma (Deception) check gets a look at it. Read against the guests at the reading, it shows two names missing: Miss Constance Sharp and Sir Reginald Stowe. A DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check on Knight: he is waiting for something, and it is not the end of his shift.

**Scene Flow.** Let the players settle, trade what they saw at the reading, and try their one bit of mischief (see Running the Questioning). Hawkins calls the first of them in after a few minutes. Run Scene 2.

### Scene 2: The Murder Board
***Detectives' Office (5), 1:30 AM. ~45 min***

{{descriptive
Photographs, pins and red thread cover the wall behind a long table. Across the top, in heavy capitals: THE SMILING MAN MURDERS.
}}

**What's Happening.** Hawkins questions each player about the reading: who stood where, what they saw when the lights went out, what they heard. He is honest and tired, and he believes the Skohle killing is the Smiling Man's work. He wants a description. He has never had a good one.

**Clues.** Give the players the Murder Board handout and let them look while Hawkins talks. A player who describes what they saw at the reading earns his trust; he then shows them the Case Notes on the Skull-Cuts and tells them the freshest victim, Miss Quill, is downstairs. A DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check on the board finds Dr. Pemberton's name on the suspect list, crossed out: asleep at home. The Memo: Keep It Quiet is spiked on his desk; Hawkins resents it and does not hide it. The Telegraph Flimsy sits under it.

**Scene Flow.** Hawkins asks each player the same question three ways. A player caught in a lie adds 1 to the Doom Counter. Keep the interviews short and rotate; the players not in the room are waiting in the Muster Room. When the last player has been questioned, or the Doom Counter reaches 6, run Scene 3.

### Scene 3: The Third Bell
***Storeroom (9), Cell Block (12) and Duty Hall (2), 3 AM. ~45 min***

{{descriptive
Somewhere in the city a bell strikes three. A cold draft runs the length of the corridor from the back of the building.
}}

**What's Happening.** Merewyn Osgood's gunmen come through the side door Knight left off the latch, one gunman for each player, less one. They are here to kill Benjamin Payne in his cell and leave him for Wystan Aldous to find. They will shoot anyone between them and the cells. The constables fall back. Hawkins fights. Till locks his door. Knight "tries to help," and pulls a bystander in front of every shot aimed at him.

**Clues.** The Crumpled Note is in Knight's pocket. The Torn Ledger Page is in his desk. The Cell Log Slip at the Turnkey's Desk (11) shows Knight booked Payne himself. The Sealed Osgood Writ is in Till's drawer, unsigned.

**Scene Flow.** Payne, if freed, runs or bargains. If the players save him, Brasshold owes them. Run Scene 4 when the gunmen bring out the fire.

### Scene 4: Pushed Down
***Stair (10) to Stair Hall (13), 3:15 AM. ~10 min***

{{descriptive
Burning lamp oil spreads across the flagstones. Smoke rolls along the ceiling toward the stair.
}}

**What's Happening.** The gunmen cannot reach Payne quickly, so they burn their way in. A Molotov lays a wall of fire across the Duty Hall (2) and a grenade brings down the cell-block door. The way to the street is fire; the open way is the Stair (10). Payne bolts down it. Hawkins, wounded, goes after him.

**Scene Flow.** Drive the players down, do not drag them. Use the fire, Burst Fire into the hall, and the people they want to protect going below. If the players defeat the gunmen before the fire, the Associate Coroner screams from the morgue (Doom 9) and Hawkins goes down to look. Either way the players end up at the foot of the stair. Run Scene 5.

#### Hawkins's Questions

| He asks | An honest answer gives |
|:--|:--|
| Who brought you to the reading? | He checks it against the blotter. |
| Where were you when the lights went out? | Who stood nearest Skohle. |
| What did you see in the dark? | His trust, and the Case Notes. |
| Did anyone leave the room? | Sharp and Stowe are missing. |
| What is a Pale Journal page? | He writes the words down. |
| Do you know a Dr. Pemberton? | "Asleep at home. Crossed off." |

### Scene 5: The Dance
***Autopsy Room (15) and the morgue, 3:30 AM. Combat***

{{descriptive
Six stone slabs stand in two rows under hanging lamps. On the far slab a sheet lies on the floor.
}}

**What's Happening.** Miss Quill is standing. Her neck has drawn out into a long pale lash with nothing at the end of it, and she is dancing. The Dancing Spinster acts after every player's turn. She walks walls and ceilings and never falls.

**Clues.** The Coroner's File on Miss Quill is in the Coroner's Office (16). The Coroner's Med Kit there can treat the Enfeebling Nox her Sapping Jig leaves. In the Laboratory (17): lamp oil, matches, and a cold-iron poker.

**Scene Flow.** This is not a fight to win. The Spinster moves only 10 feet at a time; players who run can outpace her on the floor, but not on the walls. Reward the players who get the wounded up the stair, set the morgue alight, or slam the Cold Room door between her and them. The scene ends when the players are out of the building or she is down.

### Scene 6: Aftermath
***North Harbor Police Headquarters, dawn. ~15 min***

**What's Happening.** The fire brigade puts out the Duty Hall. Captain Till takes charge, sends the witnesses home, and tells them they saw an Osgood raid and nothing else. The Spinster is gone if the players did not destroy her.

**Clues.** The Evidence Tag points to Payne's effects in the cage in the Evidence Room (4): an envelope of Brasshold money and a plain calling card. Anything the players picked up tonight goes home with them unless they are careless.

### Resolution

**Outcome Ladder.**

- **Total success.** The players survive the Spinster, save Payne and Hawkins, and leave with the Murder Board read and the station's papers in their pockets.
- **Mixed.** The players get out, but Payne or Hawkins dies, or the papers burn.
- **Total failure.** The players flee with nothing but what they remember of the Murder Board.

**Rewards.** What they read on the Murder Board. Any papers they carried out. Wystan Aldous's gratitude if Payne lives. Hawkins's trust if he does.

**Insight Cue.** The Dancing Spinster is a Mythos exposure. Use the Spinster's Insight in the Bestiary.

**Foreshadowing.** The Telegraph Flimsy reports a man of science gone missing; it is a thread for later. Sharp and Stowe, missing from the blotter, are leads in Episode Two.

**Threads and Dead Ends.**

- **The Murder Board.** The live thread. Pemberton's name is on it, crossed out.
- **Miss Quill.** What she asked the librarians, and who answered.
- **The Osgood war.** Merewyn hit Brasshold tonight. Aldous will answer.
- **Sergeant Knight.** Bought. If the players expose him, Till buries it.
- **The Memo.** "The Commander" is not Till. No one here will say who.

### Reading the Board
**The victims.** Professor Lydia Harrow (language), Dr. Emil Vey (the stars), Margaret Sloane (an artifact), Reverend Alistair Crowe (the rites). Each studied one piece of the same mystery.

**The method.** No tool marks. Each skull separated along its seams. Different parts of the brain taken from each, matching what each victim knew.

**The witnesses.** A flat black figure with a white smile and tears. It folds under doors and climbs walls.

**The timeline.** Every killing between 1:40 and 3:15 in the morning, while the city sleeps.

**The suspects.** A list of physicians, all crossed out. Dr. Pemberton among them: asleep at home, confirmed by his servant.

**What the police missed.** It wants what the victims remembered, not what they wrote. It moves through connected shadow and avoids strong light. Every murder happened while Pemberton slept.

**For the DM.** Let the players reach these conclusions themselves. Hawkins believes the Surgeon Theory.

### Running the Fight

#### The Gunmen
One gunman for each player, less one. Each turn a gunman fires the Tommy gun twice, or sprays a 10-foot cube with Burst Fire (DC 15 Dexterity), or throws a Molotov (a 15-foot wall of fire) or a grenade. They came for Payne. Players standing between the gunmen and Cell 9 draw the fire. When Payne is dead or out of reach, the survivors fall back through the side door.

#### The Constables
Two awake, two asleep in the Bunk Room (7). They Hit the Deck and Fall Back; they hold doors, they do not charge guns. Hawkins fights from cover with his snub revolver. Knight uses Grab a Bystander on anyone near him.

#### The Spinster
She takes one action after each player's turn and only moves on her own. Every action moves her 10 feet at most, across floors, walls and ceilings alike. Sapping Jig leaves an Enfeebling Nox; the Coroner's Med Kit (16) treats it. Drink Deep heals her by half the damage it deals, so a player she has coiled is a player to rescue first.

#### Ways Out
The Stair (10) is the only way up. The Cold Room (14) has a heavy door. Lamp oil from the Laboratory (17) burns.
