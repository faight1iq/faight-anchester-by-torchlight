{{atbl-keeper-summary
##### The Killing Ground
The evidence and the changed men shipped north ahead of the party, into the Battle of Paschendale. The serum turned the volunteers into jackal-headed warriors, and every early sign called the project a success. Then they tasted blood. They went berserk. They killed the enemy, their allies, and one another. Only one soldier kept his mind, Sergeant Freitman Bay. The rumor on the field is that the Special Police took him away in chains, in the armored coach that carried off Colonel Standard and Dr. Pemberton. The man in chains was Corporal Aberdeen. Bay slipped them and fled south into the wilderness after his bride, and now everyone is hunting him. The party arrives to a handful of shell-shocked regulars and a field full of the dead.
}}

### Timeline
{{noIndent
**Fifteen years ago.** A collector had the Pale Journals stolen from the Temple of Anhur in Sai Abbadon.

**Two years ago.** Zeshagu warriors joined Sous Terreborgue and took Sai Abbadon.

**One year ago.** Zeshagu's dragons and Sous Terreborgue's warships sealed Anchester. The collector had the Journals copied, and pages leaked into the city.

**Six months ago.** The Special Police secretly began building super-soldiers from something found in one of the stolen Journals.

**Three months ago.** Academics were found dead, skulls opened above the brow. The wards named the killer the Smiling Man.

**These past weeks.** The players hunted the Smiling Man to the Garrison and seized the evidence the Special Police were shipping north.

**Yesterday.** At Paschendale the serum turned the volunteers, and they slaughtered the enemy, their allies, and one another.

**Today.** The players reach the battlefield to take up the trail.
}}

\column

### Episode Structure
A hex crawl across the drowned battlefield. The players arrive at the Anchester line, hear the rumor of the coach, and go out onto the field to find what is left of the volunteers.

Each hex is 100 yards across. When the party enters a hex, roll on the Hex Table for what is there: the dead, the living, salvage, wreckage, ground, the Abhorrent, clues, or quiet dread. About one hex in seven holds one of the six horrors, the two Epic Bosses included, and about one in six holds nothing. Everything on the table is random, the Pale Journal page and the leads to Bay included.

The DM can put a finger on the scale: place any find or horror instead of rolling for it.

The episode ends when the party leaves the field with what it found, or when the field drives them off.

\column

### The Doom Counter
**Start:** equal to the party's number of Dread Insights.

**Add 1 Doom** for every three hexes crossed, for every gunshot or flare, and the first time blood is spilled in a hex.

::

#### Escalating Doom

| Mark | Escalation |
|:---:|:-----|
| 3 | Fog. The party sees only the hex it is in. |
| 6 | Each hex also holds a horror on a d6 roll of 1 or 2. |
| 9 | Rain floods the craters. All standing water holds Unholy Mackerel. |
| 12 | The Gallows hunts the party and enters their next hex. |

::
#### Manifesting Doom

| Cost | Doom |
|:-:|:-----|
| 1 | Mythos Disadvantage |
| 1 | Murphy's Law |
| 2 | The Duckboards Give Way |
| 2 | Cut Off |
| 3 | The Dead Rise |
| 5 | Activate one Dread Ability |

:::

#### Reading the Map
Each hex is 100 yards across. The sea and the sandbars lie to the northwest; Sous Terreborgue wants the long beach for a landing, and the Anchester Army dug in to stop it. Wooded high ground rings the field. Everything between is marsh, flooded craters, and channels, with duckboard tracks the only dry way across.

\column

#### Moving on the Field
Off the duckboards, a hex takes twice as long to cross, and a character who leaves the track in standing water risks the Unholy Mackerel once the Doom Counter reaches 9. Woods on the high ground give cover and slow the horrors that drag themselves.

#### Fights on the Field
When a horror turns up, run the fight on one of the six battlefield maps in Foundry (04.03.01 to 04.03.06). Pick the one whose craters and trenches suit the hex.

\column

{{dm-sidebar
#### Doom
**Mythos Disadvantage.** Disadvantage on one roll.

**Murphy's Law.** A rifle jams, a flare fails, a boot sticks fast.

**The Duckboards Give Way.** A player drops into a flooded crater, prone in water to the chest.

**Cut Off.** Wire or water blocks the way forward; the party must go around.

**The Dead Rise.** A Grafted Reservist stands up from the nearest corpse.

**Activate one Dread Ability.** Trigger a Dread Insight already held by a player.
}}

### Scene 1: The Line
***The Anchester line, south of the field. Grey dawn. ~20 min***

{{descriptive
A trench line runs along the ridge above the field. Below it, the ground is water and mud as far as the fog allows.
}}

**What's Happening.** A handful of shell-shocked regulars hold the line. Their officers are dead or gone. The Special Police left yesterday in an armored coach, with Colonel Standard and Dr. Pemberton inside.

**Clues.** Any regular will tell the party the rumor: the Special Police took the one sane survivor away in chains. They will point the coach's road north and back toward the city. None of them went out onto the field after the volunteers turned, and none will go now.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Insight DC 12.** The regulars repeat what they were told, not what they saw.

**Persuasion DC 14.** A regular shows the party the duckboard track onto the field.
}}

**Scene Flow.** When the party steps off the line, run Scene 2.

### Scene 2: The Field
***Paschendale. Day. ~2 hours***

**What's Happening.** The party crosses the field hex by hex. Roll on the Hex Table for every hex entered. Track the Doom Counter.

**Clues.** The finds under Clues and Leads (57 to 64) tell the true story: the coach doubled back south, the manacles were wrenched open, and bare footprints run south into the wilderness. The map case (30) holds a Pale Journal page.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Survival DC 13.** Each hex crossed off the duckboards. On a failure, add 1 Doom.

**Investigation DC 15.** On a Clues and Leads find. On a success, the party reads the true story in it: the ruts double back, the manacles were wrenched open.
}}

**Scene Flow.** When the table turns up a horror, run Scene 3.

\column

### Scene 3: The Abhorrent
***Any hex. ~30 min each***

**What's Happening.** One of the six horrors is in the hex. The Grafted Reservists come in numbers; the Questing Remnants come to blood; the Unholy Mackerel own the water. The Unholy Cow and The Gallows are Epic Bosses.

**Clues.** Every horror is a leaving of the serum. Abhorrent flesh outlives its host and reaches for a new one.

**Scene Flow.** Return to Scene 2. A party that runs may lose the horror only by leaving the hex.

### Scene 4: Leaving the Field
***The edge of the field. ~15 min***

**What's Happening.** The party leaves the field with whatever it found. If it found the footprints, the ring, or the letter, it knows Bay went south toward the city on foot. If not, it leaves believing the rumor of the chained man in the coach.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Survival DC 15.** With the footprints (58) found, the party follows them to the road south. Bay is on foot, headed for the city.
}}

**Scene Flow.** Episode Five begins in Anchester, with Bay loose in the city.

### Resolution

**Outcome Ladder.**

- **Total success.** The party finds the trail south and the page, and leaves before Doom 12.
- **Mixed.** The party finds the trail or the page, not both.
- **Total failure.** The party leaves believing the rumor, with nothing in hand.
- **Pyrrhic.** The Gallows comes for them, and someone stays on the field.

**Rewards.** The Pale Journal page, if found. Salvage from the table: starmetal rounds, Halloran's Salts, Dr. Boonsboro's Nervine.

**Insight Cue.** The first player to look into the pit of failures (51) or face The Gallows makes a Wisdom save against short-term madness.

**Foreshadowing.** The armored coach is already back in Anchester, and so is the man in chains.

\column

**Threads and Dead Ends.**

- **Sergeant Bay.** Fled south. Episode Five.
- **Corporal Aberdeen.** In the coach, in chains. The party does not know yet.
- **The Garrison Times.** The city will hear Paschendale was a victory.

#### Running the Living
The living on the field (11 to 20) are the party's best source of the true story. Each knows one piece of it. Calm them, and they talk; frighten them, and they repeat the rumor.

#### Running the Dead
The Abhorrent flesh is a parasite. Any corpse the party passes may be what rises when the Doom Counter reaches The Dead Rise.

{{dm-sidebar
#### Not on the Field
**Sergeant Freitman Bay.** The one volunteer who kept his mind. Fled south on foot after his bride, Claudia. Episode Five.

**Corporal Aberdeen.** The man in chains in the coach. Embraced the serum and did the real killing. The party does not know.

**Colonel Standard and Dr. Pemberton.** Gone in the armored coach, back toward the city.
}}
