{{atbl-keeper-summary
##### A Father's Bargain
Colonel Leopold Haeint, a retired army surgeon, could not save his dying daughter Anabelle. He had a mummy smuggled out of occupied Sai Abbadon, a war-priest of the Temple of Anhur set to guard the god's dead, and cut it apart in his tower laboratory to steal its deathless principle. Anabelle died anyway, but she did not leave: her spirit is held in a tank of alchemical fluid. The mummy woke, killed the Colonel and his wife, laid them out on slabs, and took up its post over the child. Now the Clarion Society wants to know why the Colonel has gone silent.
}}

### Timeline
{{noIndent
**Fifteen years ago.** A collector had the Pale Journals stolen from the Temple of Anhur in Sai Abbadon and brought to Anchester.

**Two years ago.** Zeshagu and Sous Terreborgue took Sai Abbadon. A year later their dragons and warships put Anchester under siege.

**This spring.** Anabelle Haeint falls ill with a wasting sickness. Every physician in the city gives up on her.

**Weeks ago.** Colonel Haeint obtains a page of the Pale Journals and has the Sentinel smuggled out of Sai Abbadon.

**Days ago.** Anabelle dies in the tank and lingers. The Sentinel wakes, kills the Colonel and his wife, and darkens the house.

**Today.** Bryony Thatch asks the players to find out why the Colonel has gone silent.
}}

\column

### Episode Structure
A dark house and the truth inside it. The players come looking for a missing man and find a house with every lamp smashed. The house is the episode: they search it room by room, and the clues turn the story from "a monster killed everyone" into "the monster is guarding the child."

At the top of the house they find Anabelle in her tank, able to speak while the fluid holds her. The ghosts of her parents can be called to testify. The Sentinel of Anhur walks the house and senses every living thing in it.

There are two ways to end it. The players can light the tower and destroy the Sentinel, a brutal fight. Or they can help Anabelle finish crossing over. With its charge gone, the guardian stands down. The Pale Journal page in the Colonel's safe tells them which is right.

#### Running the House
The Sentinel's post is the Tower Laboratory (24). Until the Doom Counter reaches 6 it stays there, and the players can search the lower floors freely, with only the dark and the drag marks for company.

It senses every living creature within 120 feet through walls and darkness. The players cannot hide from it, but it does not come for them unless they reach for the tank, or the Doom Counter says otherwise.

### Scene 1: The Dark House
***Front Porch (1), dusk. ~15 min***

{{descriptive
A three-storey house stands at the end of the lane behind an iron gate. Every window is dark, and a narrow tower rises from the roof.
}}

**What's Happening.** Colonel Leopold Haeint, a retired army surgeon and friend of Major Lyle Wakes, has stopped answering letters. Wakes is in hospital and Erasmus Byrgenwright will not leave his research, so Bryony Thatch, the youngest member of the Clarion Society, asks the players to look in on him. No one answers the door. It is not locked.

**Clues.** Bryony has the Colonel's last letter to Wakes (Clue 29): he writes that he has "found a way" and asks Wakes to come see. She knows the household is only the Colonel, his wife, and their sick daughter.

**Scene Flow.** Bryony comes along if the players let her. She is brave and useless in a fight. Run Scene 2 when the players step inside.

### Scene 2: Searching the House
***The whole house, evening. ~90 min***

{{descriptive
The hall smells of lamp oil and something older, like dust from a sealed room. Glass crunches underfoot.
}}

**What's Happening.** The house is dark and silent. The Sentinel is in the tower and knows the players are here. Let them search room by room using Map Locations and Found in the House.

**Clues.** Each room lists its clues. Reveal a clue when a player searches the right room; a DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check finds the smaller ones. The safe in the Study (14) needs the combination from the family Bible (3) or a DC 18 Dexterity check with thieves' tools.

**Scene Flow.** Add 1 to the Doom Counter for each hour of searching and for every loud noise. When the players have the story's broad shape, run Scene 3.

\column

### Scene 3: Piecing It Together
***Anywhere, as the clues come together. ~20 min***

**What's Happening.** This is the turn of the episode. The early clues read like a massacre: smashed lamps, drag marks, a missing family. The later ones show a guardian: it carried the dead upstairs and laid them out, it stood watch over the tank, it did not harm the girl.

**Clues.** The turning point is any of Clues 16, 17 or 18. The resolution is in the Pale Journal page (the handout), which says the crossed-over can only be released, never revived.

**Scene Flow.** Ask the players what they think happened before they climb to the attic. Their answer decides how they walk into Scene 4.

{{dm-sidebar
#### Doom
**Mythos Disadvantage.** Disadvantage on one roll: the hand shakes, the mind stalls.

**Murphy's Law.** A match will not strike, a floorboard cracks, a door sticks.

**Lights Out, Somebody Screams.** One carried light goes out. The Sentinel knows where everyone is; the players do not.

**A Door, My Old Nemesis.** A door the players need is jammed or locked.

**Cut Off.** The Sentinel stands between the players and the stair.

**From the Shadows.** The Sentinel makes one Rotting Fist attack against a player in dim light or darkness, then withdraws.

**Activate one Dread Ability.** Trigger a Dread Insight already held by a player.
}}

### Scene 4: Anabelle
***Tower Laboratory (24). ~20 min***

{{descriptive
A tall glass tank stands in a frame of brass and wire, humming. A thin girl floats inside, eyes open, hair drifting in the green fluid.
}}

**What's Happening.** Anabelle Haeint died in the tank and did not leave. While the fluid holds her she can speak, faintly, through the glass. If she is lifted out she dissolves into sludge and is gone. The Sentinel stands in the corner and does not move unless someone reaches for the tank.

**Clues.** Anabelle remembers the pain stopping, her father weeping, and "the tall man in the wrappings" who sends everyone away. She asks for her mother. She is tired. She wants to go to sleep and cannot.

**Scene Flow.** Any player who touches the tank, the valves, or the girl draws the Sentinel at once (Scene 6, The Fight). A player who talks to her can ask what she wants. Her answer is the Mercy.

### Scene 5: The Ghosts
***Slab Room (23), or anywhere in the house. ~20 min***

**What's Happening.** Dr. and Mrs. Haeint can be called back as ghosts to testify: by a ritual from the Colonel's notes, by a cleric's magic, or by speaking to them at their slabs. They are confused and grieving, and each holds half the story.

**Clues.** The Colonel confesses the smuggling, the page, and the experiments, and admits he cannot let her go. Mrs. Haeint saw the Sentinel kneel by the tank. She begs the players to let her daughter rest.

**Scene Flow.** The Colonel resists the Mercy. If the players move to release Anabelle, he possesses one of them (DC 13 Charisma) to stop it. Mrs. Haeint helps.

### Scene 6: Resolution
***Tower Laboratory (24). Set-piece***

**The Fight.** The players light the tower and destroy the Sentinel. In darkness it resists everything but fire and radiant damage and attacks with advantage. Bright light strips that away. Use Running the Sentinel. When it falls it stays down.

**The Mercy.** The players help Anabelle finish crossing over: by her mother's voice, by a prayer, or by draining the tank at her request, not against her will. As she goes, the Sentinel kneels. With its charge gone, it crumbles to dust and sand.

**Either Way.** The house is quiet. The page is theirs.

### Resolution

**Outcome Ladder.**

- **Total success.** The players release Anabelle with her parents' blessing. The Sentinel stands down. They leave with the page and the whole story.
- **Mixed.** The players destroy the Sentinel by fire and light. Anabelle is released or lost in the fight. They leave with the page.
- **Total failure.** The players flee the house with the Rotting Hex in their blood and the page left behind.
- **Pyrrhic.** The players lift Anabelle out to "save" her. She dissolves. The Sentinel fights to the end.

**Rewards.** The page of the Pale Journals, "Of the Borrowed Spark." Studying it teaches Curse Transfer and the formula for the Revitalizing Tincture.

**Insight Cue.** The Sentinel is a Mythos exposure. The first player to study the page loses 1d6 Sanity or makes a Wisdom save against short-term madness.

**Foreshadowing.** Days later, Colonel Standard of the Special Police seizes the Colonel's experiments and every note left in the house. The serum begins here.

**Threads and Dead Ends.**

- **The page.** The live thread. It is the second page the players have seen.
- **The smuggler.** Shipping papers name a fast boat out of Sai Abbadon.
- **Colonel Standard.** He knew about the tank before the players did.
- **Major Wakes.** He will want to know what became of his friend.

### Running the Sentinel

#### Umbral Fortitude
In dim light or darkness the Sentinel resists all damage except fire and radiant, attacks with advantage, and its Dreadful Glare and Blasphemous Word rise to DC 18. In bright light it loses the resistance, attacks with disadvantage, and its Rotting Fist deals no necrotic damage. Until the room is lit, treat it as having about 600 hit points.

#### Life-Sense
It knows where every living creature within 120 feet is, through walls and darkness, and which of them has the fewest hit points. Only a warded threshold, three feet of stone, or a sheet of lead blocks it. Anabelle does not register. The Lead-Foil Ward Charm, Warding Salt and Grave-Dust Sachet hide a player from it.

#### Guttering Gloom
A legendary action dims every ordinary light within 30 feet one step. The Oil of Quenchless Flame and the Magnesium Flare are the answers.

#### The Rotting Hex
Its Glare and its Blasphemous Word lay a Hex on Constitution. Each further failure while cursed climbs one tier: Hex, Nox, Pox, Curse, Damnation. Only once per turn on the same creature. Its Rotting Fist also carries mummy rot (DC 16 Constitution).

#### Tactics
It is a guardian, not a hunter. It wants the living away from Anabelle. It keeps the tower dark with Guttering Gloom and *darkness*, and uses Whirlwind of Dust to slip past the front line back to the tank. Anyone reaching for the tank is its first target.

#### Tuning for Level 5
As written it is deadly. Treat it as a set piece: the win is light and mercy, not damage. To soften it, drop its hit points to about 200 and its Legendary Resistance to 2/day, but keep Umbral Fortitude. Fire and radiant damage cut through even in darkness.

#### The Players' Dilemma
They must light the room to hurt it, while keeping it off the one spirit it exists to protect. Every round spent fighting near the tank is a round the tank can break. The wiser path is to stop endangering Anabelle and help her go.

#### The Revitalizing Tincture
With the page and a short rest at an alchemist's bench, a player can brew a draught that pulls a creature back from death's edge: stabilize and heal substantially, or lift one tier of a wasting curse or disease. The decay must go somewhere. The brewer chooses a vessel, a willing ally, an enemy, or themselves, and that vessel takes a Rotting Hex of the same tier.

#### Curse Transfer
Studying the page teaches Curse Transfer: push a weaker curse onto another creature and promote your own one tier. The page names it the true mechanism of revitalization: you do not heal the rot, you move it.

**THE COLONEL'S LOG**

**Day 9.** The principle holds in the tissue. I have it. I have it.

**Day 11.** She is gone. She is not gone. She looks at me through the glass.

**Day 12.** It has taken station by the tank. It will let no one near her. Not even me.

**Day 13.** If I lift her out she will not survive it. If I leave her she will not live. I cannot

*(The entry ends.)*
}}

{{classified,bot
**MRS. HAEINT'S DIARY**

It killed him. I saw it. And then it went to her tank and knelt, like a soldier at a grave, and it has not moved since. It is not a monster. It is a sentry. God help me, it is guarding my girl.
}}

L.H.

## Cast
}}

{{castMember
##### Bryony Thatch
*Clarion Society newcomer, Harengon, young.*

AC15; HP3; Rabbit Gun+5/2d8+3; Hurl Something+1/1d8
{stats}

*Description*: Long ears, bright eyes, a borrowed coat a size too big, a notebook full of questions.

*Mannerisms*: Wonders aloud at everything. Asks "but why?" one time too many.

*Intent*: Brought in about the same time as the players and thrilled to be here. Wants to prove the Society right to trust her. Her curiosity runs well ahead of her caution.
}}

{{castMember
##### Anabelle Haeint
*The Colonel's daughter, crossed over, in the tank.*

AC15; HP3; No weapon
{stats}

*Description*: Thin, pale, hair drifting in green fluid, eyes open.

*Mannerisms*: Speaks faintly through the glass. Asks for her mother. Tired.

*Intent*: Wants to sleep and cannot. If lifted out of the fluid she dissolves to sludge and is gone.
}}

\column

{{castMember
##### Dr. Leopold Haeint
*Ghost of the Colonel, retired army surgeon.*

AC11; HP45; Withering Touch+5/4d6+3 necrotic; Horrifying Visage, Possession DC13
{stats}

*Description*: Shirt-sleeves, a surgeon's apron, hands that will not stop shaking.

*Mannerisms*: Explains himself before anyone asks.

*Intent*: Cannot let her go. Possesses a player to stop the Mercy if he must.
}}

{{castMember
##### Mrs. Haeint
*Ghost of the Colonel's wife.*

AC11; HP45; Withering Touch+5/4d6+3 necrotic; Horrifying Visage, Possession DC13
{stats}

*Description*: Nightgown and shawl, hair unpinned, eyes on the tower stair.

*Mannerisms*: Quiet. Looks past the players to the tower.

*Intent*: Wants her daughter to rest. Helps the players release her.
}}

## Horrors

{{castMember
##### The Sentinel of Anhur
*Medium undead (Mythos), mummy lord set piece. See Bestiary.*

AC17; HP300; Speed 20; Rotting Fist+9/2d6+4 plus 6d6 necrotic and mummy rot (DC16 Con); Dreadful Glare DC16 (18 in darkness); Legendary Resistance 3/day
{stats}

*Description*: A war-priest of Anhur in rotted temple wrappings, cut open at the chest and stitched shut with surgical wire. Its dead eyes catch no light.

*Mannerisms*: Stands motionless until the living come near its charge. Moves in a hiss of grave-sand.

*Intent*: Guard Anabelle. Keep the living away from the tank. Kill anyone who reaches for her.

**Weaknesses.** Fire and radiant damage; bright light (strips Umbral Fortitude).

**Immunities.** Necrotic, poison; nonmagical weapons.

**Legendary Actions.** Attack; Guttering Gloom; Blasphemous Word (2); Whirlwind of Dust (2).

**When Destroyed.** It does not rise again.
}}
