{{atbl-keeper-summary
##### The Missing Bride
Freitman Bay lived through the last episode, and his wife Claudia is still missing. She is on the run with her friend Evelyn Ridgely, carrying Bay's unborn child. The Special Police are hunting both women for the Clairvoyant, the hidden power the state answers to, and they are emptying the city of anyone who could help: Claudia's parents, a perfumer, a university professor. The papers call the vanished a Pixie Dust ring. The Smiling Man is killing again. The party follows the trail of the two women, and every lead runs toward the Special Police safehouses where Dr. Pemberton sleeps.
}}

### Timeline
{{noIndent
**Fifteen years ago.** A collector had the Pale Journals stolen from the Temple of Anhur in Sai Abbadon.

**One year ago.** Zeshagu's dragons and Sous Terreborgue's warships sealed Anchester. Pages of the Journals leaked into the city.

**Six months ago.** The Special Police began building super-soldiers from a serum found in one of the Journals.

**Weeks ago.** At Paschendale the serum turned the volunteers into monsters. The city was told it was a victory.

**Days ago.** Freitman Bay came home hunting his wife. The players saved him. Claudia and Evelyn were already in hiding.

**Today.** The Special Police are closing on the two women, and the Smiling Man has opened another skull.
}}

\column

### Episode Structure
A manhunt with one goal: find Claudia. The players move freely across the wards. Each location holds a clue and points to one or two others. None of them leads straight to Claudia; the trail is bait, and every thread runs toward the people hunting her.

While the players search, the Special Police act offscreen, marked on the Doom Counter. When the Counter reaches 12, the finale happens at whichever safehouse the party is in or heading for. Claudia is already gone.

The two safehouses, Rue Blanc in Brasshold and Vine Street at Union Station, point to each other. When timing calls for it, skip the pointer and let the finale be at the safehouse the party has reached.

{{dm-sidebar
#### Doom
**Mythos Disadvantage.** Disadvantage on one roll.

**Murphy's Law.** A lamp gutters, a door sticks, a cab throws a wheel.

**Shut Door.** A witness has been warned off and will not talk without a DC 18 check.

**Plain Clothes.** Two Special Guards shadow the party to the next location.

**Disappeared.** A named witness the party has not yet met is taken. Their location is empty.

**Activate one Dread Ability.** Trigger a Dread Insight already held by a player.
}}

**Start:** equal to the party's number of Dread Insights.

**Add 1 Doom** for every location visited, every fight, and every broadsheet that reaches the Special Police.

### Running the Crawl
**Go %** is the chance a party with no lead ends up there anyway; use it to judge which places the players will reach. **Fight %** is the chance a fight breaks out during the visit; roll once per visit, or decide.

**Points To** is a breadcrumb, never a route. Each lead names a place, and a ward to find it in. Getting closer is presentation: leads run warmer, the broadsheets darken, the Counter climbs.

**The broadsheets.** Each location has a paper lying about, pinned up, or pressed into the party's hands. Hand out the page when the players pick it up. Every paper hides one clue in a side column.

#### The Enemy Clock
The players never see the Special Police at work, only what they leave behind. At Doom 3 they take the two women from the Sheets. At Doom 6 the Clairvoyant summons Colonel Standard, and Standard leaves with Claudia, leaving Dr. Pemberton to hold the safehouse with a Captain and four Special Guards. At Doom 9, with Standard gone, Pemberton falls asleep and the Smiling Man goes hunting.

#### The Safehouse Chain
Both safehouses look abandoned until Doom 12. In each, the party finds Evelyn's handbag and the name of the other safehouse scratched into a doorframe. The Smiling Man's victims turn up near both.

#### If the Players Stall
Widow Ashe and Hamilton Frederick both point to Rue Blanc. Hazel Attwater points to both safehouses. Use them as the safety valve.

#### Fights in the City
Run fights on the location's map in Foundry (06.03 to 06.15). The Special Police come in squads: a Captain or two Special Guards per player. A Hungry Crowd fills any street in the poor wards. The Watch fights in uniform and gives up easily.

{{dm-sidebar
#### Running the Clairvoyant
The players learn that the Clairvoyant exists: the Special Police answer to it, and it wants Claudia's child. They learn nothing about who it is. No location, witness, or paper in this episode says more.
}}

## Locations
}}

### Ash Ward
**The Easton House (Hamilton Row).** Claudia's parents, Alfred and Maud Easton, butler and lady's maid to the Ridgelys. The door is broken and the house turned over. The neighbors watched the Special Police take the couple at night and say nothing unless pressed. On the floor: a photograph of Claudia and Evelyn, and a paycheck signed Ridgely.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Investigation DC 12.** The paycheck and the photograph name Evelyn and the Ridgelys.

**Persuasion DC 14.** A neighbor saw the wagon's markings: Special Police.
}}

**The Sheets.** The shanty town of the poor. The two women hid here with Evelyn's friend Nancy Mallamore until the Special Police dragged them out. Bette Huerlock, a washerwoman, saw it and will tell it for a meal. A Hungry Crowd turns on anyone who looks like police.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Persuasion DC 13.** Bette describes the raid. The wagons were Union Station Watch, the men inside were not.

**Intimidation DC 15.** The crowd parts. On a failure, the Hungry Crowd attacks.
}}

### East Row
**Claudia's Perfume Shop.** Shuttered, a Special Police notice on the door. Cissy Gelena sells flowers from a cart outside and liked Claudia. She saw police come asking for two girls and heard them say "the Sheets."

**Widow Ashe.** The fortune teller the Ridgelys hired. Every time she scries for the girls, something dark slams the door on her. She has seen one thing clearly: a yard of brass and soot, a white street sign, Rue Blanc. For a séance she can try again with the party at the table; it draws the Yfelgaest, a poltergeist that throws the parlour at them.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Insight DC 14.** Widow Ashe is frightened of what she saw, not of the party.

**Arcana DC 16.** The darkness that blocks her is a shadow with a will of its own.
}}

### Meskawaki University
**Evelyn's Dorm.** Her roommate says Evelyn left to help Claudia hours before the police came, and names their friend Nancy Mallamore in the Sheets. Special Police watch the building.

**Dr. Magrat's Office.** Professor Magrat, Evelyn's supervisor, is gone. A note on his desk says the Special Police "engaged" him for an investigation.

**Hazel Attwater.** The underground journalist who prints The Worker. She hides her press in a cellar under the university and trusts no one sent by money. Win her over and she shows the report her source lifted from a Special Police courier, and gives the two addresses it names.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Persuasion DC 16.** Hazel trusts the party. On a failure, she gives them a copy of The Worker and shows them out.

**Stealth DC 14.** The party leaves the dorm without the watchers following.
}}

### North Ward
**The Special Police Garrison.** The ward the Special Police took over for their own. Desk Sergeant Cecil Frost hides behind procedure and the ledger. Colonel Standard has not been seen here since Paschendale. In the cells: shopkeepers, the Eastons, and Professor Magrat. Getting in means a squad of Special Guards and a Captain.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Deception DC 16.** Frost lets the party read the ledger: the raid on the Sheets was signed out to the Union Station Watch House.

**Stealth DC 15.** The party reaches the cells unseen.
}}

**The Ridgely House (Brownstone Villas).** Augustus and Georgiana Ridgely, rich and frantic, are paying for a private search. They will pay the party too. They name the two people they hired: Hamilton Frederick and Widow Ashe.

**The Repose.** The Coroner's Court. Assistant Coroner Aubrey Salisbury, a records man, is rattled: the skull-killings have started again, a body was found in the Meskawaki library, and the Special Police seize the bodies and close his inquests. His records show the newest bodies came from Brasshold, near Rue Blanc.

### North Harbor
**Hamilton Frederick, PI.** A hard-bitten investigator working for the Ridgelys. He has three broadsheets pinned to his wall and a source who says Standard has gone rogue. He has an address in Brasshold: a Special Police safehouse on Rue Blanc.

### Union Station
**The Watch House.** The cabs and trolleys station's police post. Sergeant Henry Sharpe's men lent their wagons to the raid on the Sheets. Standard rode with them and took two women. The driver heard him say "Vine Street." The Watch fights if the party forces the door.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Intimidation DC 14.** Sharpe gives up the driver.
}}

### The Safehouses
**Rue Blanc, Brasshold** and **Vine Street, Union Station.** Two requisitioned flats on ordinary terraced streets. Before Doom 12, each is empty: Evelyn's handbag under a bed, chains on a bedframe, and the name of the other safehouse scratched into the bedroom doorframe. At Doom 12, the one the party enters is the finale.

{{dm-sidebar
#### Roads Not Taken
**Nancy Mallamore.** Evelyn's friend in the Sheets. She hid the two women and saw them taken.

**Freitman Bay.** Alive and hunted as a witness. He is not in this episode.
}}

#### Reading the Map
Both safehouses share this plan. The front steps open into the study. A hall runs from the study past the kitchen to the parlour and both bedrooms. Every room has shuttered windows. Map: 06.04 Rue Blanc or 06.11 Vine Street in Foundry.

{{noIndent
**1. Study.** Desk, books, papers. One Special Guard. The journal is in the desk.

**2. Hall.** Runs the length of the flat.

**3. Parlour.** Three Special Guards at cards.

**4. Kitchen.** The Captain, at the table, with a view down the hall.

**5. Back bedroom.** Dr. Pemberton, asleep.

**6. Front bedroom.** Evelyn Ridgely, chained to the bed.
}}

### The Finale: The Safehouse
***Rue Blanc or Vine Street. Night. ~60 min***

{{descriptive
An ordinary terraced flat, shutters closed, a lamp lit in the kitchen. Behind one door, a man is snoring.
}}

**What's Happening.** Claudia is gone; Standard took her to the Clairvoyant. Dr. Pemberton holds the flat with a Captain and four Special Guards, Evelyn chained in the front bedroom. Pemberton is asleep. His shadow is out hunting.

**Getting In.** The players choose: the front steps or any shuttered window. Do not fix a breach point.

**Round 1.** The Special Police fight. The Captain, in the kitchen, reinforces whichever end of the flat the players hit.

**Round 2.** The Smiling Man comes home. It appears in the back bedroom at the foot of Pemberton's bed, wherever the fight is.

**Round 3.** Pemberton wakes. The moment he is awake, the Smiling Man is pulled back into his shadow and trapped there. It cannot leave while he stays awake.

**Clues.** Players who read the Smiling Man's journal (Episode Five) know the shadow walks only while the doctor sleeps. Keeping Pemberton awake keeps it trapped.

##### Skill Checks
{{noIndent
**Athletics DC 15.** Force the shutters. Daylight through a window puts the Smiling Man under Sundered by Sunlight.

**Sleight of Hand DC 14.** Pick Evelyn's chains, or take the key from the Captain.

**Medicine DC 13.** Keep Pemberton awake through the night: smelling salts, cold water, talk.
}}

**The Smiling Man.** It is not beaten by damage. It leaves when Pemberton wakes, when daylight reaches it, or when it chooses Bag Trick.

### Resolution

**Outcome Ladder.**

- **Total success.** Evelyn freed, Pemberton taken alive and kept awake, the journal in hand.
- **Mixed.** Pemberton taken, Evelyn lost, or the reverse.
- **Total failure.** The Smiling Man takes Pemberton with it into the bag. Evelyn is moved.
- **Pyrrhic.** Pemberton is taken, and a player has been Excised.

**Rewards.** The journal from the study. Evelyn, who saw Standard leave with Claudia. Pemberton, a captive who must never sleep.

**Insight Cue.** Every player who sees the Smiling Man makes the Weeping Smile save; at the end of the fight, a Mythos Event save or a Dreadful Insight.

**Foreshadowing.** Evelyn heard Standard say he was taking Claudia to the Clairvoyant.

**Threads and Dead Ends.**

- **Claudia.** In Standard's hands. Episode Seven.
- **Pemberton.** No one stays awake forever.
- **The Clairvoyant.** Real, and holding the Bride.

{{dm-sidebar
#### Keeping Him Awake
Pemberton is a man of AC 11 and 13 HP. He does not fight. He begs to sleep. Every night he is held, a player keeps watch.
}}
