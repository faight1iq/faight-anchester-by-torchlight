# E09 Back in the Village: Claremonte House

Room names follow the maps 09.06 to 09.09. Items marked (John) are your rulings; the rest is proposal for review.

## The setup (John)
- **Why the players go in:** they are looking for Claudia. Nothing in the house points to her being anywhere but the deer park.
- **The house is the dungeon.** Rooms, occupants and threats are set up; the players choose how to deal with them (sneak, talk, fight).
- **The ritual** is in the deer park beyond the lake, seen only from the second floor (Guest Bedroom, south Staff Bedroom). Finding it is the cliffhanger into E10.
- **Clues** to the ritual's location are found inside the house. Nothing outside the house points to it.
- **No innocents.** The cult sent the ordinary servants away. Everyone at the estate tonight is hostile.
- **The two women.** Two pregnant women were kept in the locked basement room. Claudia has been moved elsewhere (where is not yet decided). The other woman is at the ritual. The Clairvoyant knew the cult might have been compromised but judged the ritual too important not to attempt.

## Mythos Cultists (John)
They are not very sturdy and cast freely, even when it may kill them.

| Type | What it does | Death curse |
|---|---|---|
| **Cultist** | Poison dagger; casts Mythos curses (Pox); stats vary | None |
| **Corrupted Cultist** | Stronger and tougher; hits inflict Nox | Rises as an Aberration |
| **Cultist Elder** | Mythos spells; can become an Aberration without dying | Inflicts Nox |
| **Aberration** | Tentacled Mythos transformation | Inflicts Pox |

## Ether summons (John)
Any spell or curse cast in the house, by players or cultists, has a percentage chance (not yet set) to pull a hungry Mythos creature through. It arrives next to the caster and attacks whoever is nearest, cultist or player.

**What comes through:** roll d10 + spell level.

| d10 + level | Creature |
|---|---|
| 1-4 | Ether Ameba, small |
| 5-7 | Ether Ameba, medium |
| 8-9 | Ether Ameba, large |
| 10-11 | Flying Polyp |
| 12-13 | Dimensional Shambler |
| 14-15 | Colour Out of Space |
| 16+ | Hunting Horror |

| Creature | What it is | In the house | Build from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ether Ameba | Translucent single-celled swimmer, dog-sized to carriage-sized; what it has eaten hangs visible inside it | Engulfs and slowly digests; pours under doors and through keyholes; a hit that does not kill it splits it in two | Gelatinous cube |
| Flying Polyp | Partly invisible, made of wind | Slams doors, blows out lamps, hurls furniture | Air elemental |
