## 4. ABTL rules that every creature build must follow

- **Only Epic Bosses** (sheet column Y = "Y") go on Initiative 10, use Responses (triggered, no action cost) and have **no reactions and no bonus actions**. Everything else rolls initiative normally and uses standard reactions and bonus actions.
- Epic Boss actors in the world (confirmed by John 2026-10-16): Abhorrent Soldier, Abhorrent Rear Guard, Mrs. Lovely's Cat, the Intellect Hunters (Bealu, Gedwild, Wraec, Tweoung, Unraed, Saed), the Smiling Man, Corporal Aberdeen, Sergeant Freitman Bay, Ghastly Wake, Unholy Cow.
- **No conditions on actions** (John, 2026-10-16). Never add a "when another creature does something" proviso to an action, never write "the triggering creature" in an action, and don't add "one creature within X ft." range text (redundant or limiting). Reaction-style abilities are typed Reaction in Foundry; "(Bonus Action)" abilities are typed Bonus. Automatic riders (like the Dragonborn two-hit effects) are passive (Special), not Actions.
- Curses: tiers Hex, Nox, Pox, Curse, Damnation. Adjectives: Enfeebling (STR), Fumbling (DEX), Rotting (CON), Befuddling (INT), Tormenting (WIS), Repulsive (CHA). STR/DEX/CON curses use Con saves; INT/WIS/CHA curses use Cha saves.
- Cultist tiers built for E09 (reuse for E10 villagers if John wants): Awakened Cultist (dies to focused fire; reaction curse on its attacker), Corrupted Cultist (adds a bonus action to toughen; rises on Init 20 as an Awakened Aberration), Cultist Elder (adds spellcasting; death curse on the room; rises on Init 20 as a Corrupted Aberration). Aberrations are mind-flayer inspired.
- Every skill and weapon named in an actor's bio must be set on its Foundry sheet.
