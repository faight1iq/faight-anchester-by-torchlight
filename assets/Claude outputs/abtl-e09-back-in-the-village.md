## The House at Silverbrook
Colonel Standard is dead, and with him the players' best way to the Clairvoyant. What they took from his body still points somewhere: his Pale Journal tells them the Clairvoyant means to become a God King through the power of an outsider, and his iron gate key leads to Claremonte Gardens, a country house outside the village of Silverbrook, just east of East Row. The players are looking for Claudia Bay. Claremonte Gardens is where the cult kept two pregnant women. Tonight it has moved Claudia somewhere else and taken the other woman to a clearing in the deer park, across the lake, for a ritual. The house is held by the cultists left behind, and every spell cast inside it may pull something hungry through from the other side.

### Timeline
**Days ago.** Colonel Standard kidnapped Claudia Bay, the pregnant wife of Sergeant Freitman Bay, and delivered her to the Clairvoyant.

**Yesterday.** At the Lord of Light's gala, a dragonborn assassin killed Standard. The players took his Pale Journal and an iron gate key from his body.

**Tonight.** The cult at Claremonte Gardens has sent the household's ordinary servants away. Claudia has been moved. The Clairvoyant knew the cult might have been compromised, but judged the ritual too important not to attempt. The other woman has been taken to the deer park for the ritual, which begins at ten.

### Episode Structure
The players read the Journal and follow the key out of the city to Silverbrook. They spend a short time in the village, then go up to the estate.

The house is the dungeon. It is set up with rooms, occupants and threats, and the players decide how to deal with each one: sneak past, talk, or fight. The players are looking for Claudia. Nothing outside the house points to where she or the ritual is; the clues are all inside. Everyone at the estate tonight is a cultist.

The episode ends when the players look out of a second-floor window, see the ritual lights in the deer park across the lake, and realise where Claudia must be. She is not there. That is Episode Ten.

### The Doom Counter
**Start:** equal to the party's number of Dread Insights.

#### Manifesting Doom

| Cost | Doom |
|:-:|:-----|
| 1 | Mythos Disadvantage |
| 1 | Murphy's Law |
| 2 | Cut Off |
| 3 | Sound the Alarm! |
| 3 | Enemy Reinforcements |
| 5 | Activate one Dread Ability |

**Mythos Disadvantage.** Disadvantage on one roll.

**Murphy's Law.** A board creaks, a door sticks, a lamp gutters out.

**Cut Off.** A door is locked or a passage is blocked. The players must go around.

**Sound the Alarm!** A cultist rings the dinner gong or a bell pull. Every cultist on that floor comes.

**Enemy Reinforcements.** A cultist from the next room joins the fight.

**Activate one Dread Ability.** Trigger a Dread Insight already held by a player.

### Ether Summons
Claremonte House is thin to the outside. Any spell or curse cast inside it, by a player or a cultist, has a **__%** chance *(John to set)* to pull a hungry Mythos creature through. The creature appears next to the caster and attacks whoever is nearest, cultist or player.

The cultists know this and do not care. They know they are outmatched, and they cast anyway.

**What comes through.** Roll d10 + spell level.

## Silverbrook

*Maps: 09.02 The Green, 09.03 The Claremonte Arms, 09.04 The Churchyard, 09.05 The Lodge Gates.*

A small village a short ride east of East Row. A green, a coaching inn, a Martyr church with its brazier tower, and the lodge gates of Claremonte Gardens at the end of the lane. The players stop here briefly on the way to the estate.

### Scene 1: The Journal and the Key
***The Clarion Society, morning. ~20 min***

> Colonel Standard's Pale Journal lies open on the library table beside an iron gate key on a broken watch chain.

**What's Happening.** The players study what they took from Standard's body. The Journal tells them what the Clairvoyant wants: to become a God King through the power of an outsider. It does not say how, or who.

**Clues.** The key carries a brass tag stamped *Claremonte Gdns. Lodge* *(proposal: how the key points to the estate)*. A DC 12 Intelligence (History) check, or any Clarion Society member, places Claremonte Gardens outside Silverbrook, east of East Row.

**Scene Flow.** When the players decide to go, cut to the village. The ride takes most of the day; they arrive in the late afternoon.

### Scene 2: The Green
***The Green (09.02), late afternoon. ~15 min***

> A wide green with a pond and a pump. Cottages face it on three sides, and a lane runs off the far corner toward a pair of stone gateposts.

**What's Happening.** Children sent out from the city to live and work in the country play on the green *(John's idea; details are proposal)*. Villagers are going home for supper.

**Clues.** The children know the big house stopped taking deliveries this week, and that its servants came down the lane with their boxes. They point the players to the Claremonte Arms, where some of the servants are staying.

**Scene Flow.** Let the players go to the inn, the churchyard or straight to the lodge.

### Scene 3: The Claremonte Arms
***The Claremonte Arms, taproom (09.03), evening. ~30 min***

> A low-beamed taproom with a fire at one end and settles along the walls. Pewter mugs hang over the bar.

**What's Happening.** Two of the servants sent away from Claremonte Gardens, a housemaid and a groom, are drinking here on the month's wages they were paid to leave *(proposal)*. They do not know about the cult.

**Clues.** The servants can describe the house and its ways in: the front door, the library and study garden doors, the servants' entrance, the coal chute and the walled-garden door. They say the butler and the housekeeper kept the rest of the staff on. A DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) check gets the housemaid to admit she was forbidden the cellar and heard a woman crying down there, which the housekeeper told her was a cat. This tells the players to look inside the house; it does not tell them where Claudia is now.

**Scene Flow.** The servants know nothing of the deer park or of a ritual. Do not let them.

### Scene 4: The Churchyard
***The Churchyard (09.04), dusk. ~15 min***

> A churchyard of leaning stones under old yews. The church tower rises above it, and a flame burns in the iron brazier at its top.

**What's Happening.** A quiet place, and an optional one. A priest of the Martyr tends the brazier *(proposal)*.

**Clues.** The priest says the household from the big house stopped coming to services months ago. From the tower the players can see the roofs of Claremonte House above the trees, with lamps lit below stairs and the main rooms dark. The deer park is hidden by the house and the woods.

**Scene Flow.** If the players skip this scene, nothing is lost.

### Scene 5: The Lodge Gates
***The Lodge Gates (09.05), night. ~15 min***

> Two stone gateposts and a pair of iron gates, chained shut. A lamp burns in the window of the lodge beside them.

**What's Happening.** The gatekeeper, a **Cultist**, sits at the lodge window. Standard's key opens the gate chain.

**Scene Flow.** The players may open the gate with the key, climb the wall, or deal with the gatekeeper. Once they are through, move to the grounds map and run the crawl.

## Claremonte Gardens

*Maps: 09.06 Grounds, 09.07 Ground Floor, 09.08 Second Floor, 09.09 Basement.*

**Ways in.** A the front door through the porch. B the library garden door. C the servants' entrance. D the coal chute into the coal cellar. E the walled-garden door. F the study garden door.

**Running the Crawl.** Every cultist here is hostile. None of them surrender, and all of them cast. Let the players choose how to deal with each room. A body found, a gong rung or a creature loose raises the Doom Counter by 1 *(proposal)*.

**The Cultists.** Mythos Cultists are not very sturdy, and killing one sets off a death curse.

| Type | What it does | Death curse |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Cultist** | Poison dagger; casts Pox curses | None |
| **Corrupted Cultist** | Stronger and tougher; hits inflict Nox | Rises as an Aberration |
| **Cultist Elder** | Mythos spells; can become an Aberration without dying | Inflicts Nox |
| **Aberration** | Tentacled Mythos transformation | Inflicts Pox |

**G2. Terrace and Parterre.** Flagged terrace, planters, iron benches, box-hedged beds, the fountain and the Long Walk. Two **Corrupted Cultists**, the watchmen, walk a circuit of the house with a shuttered lantern.

### Scene 6: Into the House
***Claremonte Gardens, night. ~90 min***

> A dark H-shaped house of grey stone. Lamps burn in the low windows of the east wing; the rest of the house is black.

**What's Happening.** The players are inside the grounds and looking for Claudia. The household is awake below stairs and getting ready to go down to the ritual.

**Clues.** The study map (6), the day book (10), the linen book (13) and the locked room (32) all point to the deer park. None of them names Claudia.

**Scene Flow.** Run the house from the map locations. Let the players pick their way in and their route. Each cultist fights to the death and casts freely; every cast may bring something through (see Ether Summons).

### Scene 7: The Locked Room
***The Basement (32), night. ~30 min***

> An iron-banded door at the end of the vaulted passage. Two figures stand in front of it.

**What's Happening.** The locked room is the one place the players can be sure Claudia was held. The door is guarded by a Corrupted Cultist and an Aberration.

**Clues.** Inside, two beds, not one. Both are empty.

**Scene Flow.** The key is on the housekeeper (13). Without it the door can be forced *(John to set the DC)*. Killing the Corrupted Cultist raises it as a second Aberration.

### Scene 8: The Window
***The Guest Bedroom (15) or the south Staff Bedroom (26), a little before ten. ~10 min***

> A window over the dark lawns. Beyond them the lake, and beyond the lake the black line of the deer park.

**What's Happening.** Lanterns are moving among the trees on the far side of the lake. They gather in a clearing. The ritual has begun.

**Scene Flow.** End the episode here. The players believe Claudia is in that clearing. She is not. Episode Ten begins at the ritual.

### Resolution

**Outcome Ladder.** *(proposal)*

- **Total success.** The players reach the window with the Doom Counter low and the house cleared or bypassed. They have time to reach the clearing.
- **Mixed.** The players reach the window after a long fight. The ritual is well under way.
- **Total failure.** The players reach the window with the house in an uproar and creatures loose behind them.

**Rewards.** *(John to set.)*

**Insight Cue.** *(John to set.)*

**Foreshadowing.** The ritual in the deer park. Claudia's whereabouts stay unknown.

**Threads and Dead Ends.**

- **The deer park.** The live thread. Episode Ten.
- **Claudia.** Moved before the players arrived. Nothing in this episode says where.
- **The dismissed servants.** A dead end. They know the house, nothing about the cult.

## Horrors
*Stat blocks to come.*

#### Cultist
Poison dagger. Casts Pox curses. Stats vary. No death curse.

#### Corrupted Cultist
Stronger and tougher. Hits inflict Nox. **Death curse:** rises as an Aberration.

#### Cultist Elder
Mythos spells. Can become an Aberration without dying. **Death curse:** inflicts Nox.

#### Aberration
A tentacled Mythos transformation. **Death curse:** inflicts Pox.

#### Ether Ameba
Translucent single-celled swimmer from the ether, dog-sized to carriage-sized; what it has eaten hangs visible inside it. Engulfs and slowly digests. Pours under doors and through keyholes. A hit that does not kill it splits it in two. *Build from: gelatinous cube.*

#### Flying Polyp
Partly invisible, made of wind. Slams doors, blows out lamps, hurls furniture. *Build from: air elemental.*

#### Dimensional Shambler
A loose-skinned thing that walks between worlds. Grabs a victim and steps out of the world with them; the victim reappears rounds later somewhere else in the house. *Build from: star spawn mangler.*

#### Colour Out of Space
A shifting light with no name. Drains everything it touches grey and brittle. *Build from: wraith.*

#### Hunting Horror
A great black flying serpent. Fills the hall, the stair hall and the gallery. Flees light. *Build from: young black dragon.*
